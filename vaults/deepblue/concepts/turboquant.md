# TurboQuant

**Summary**: TurboQuant is a quantization method for high‑dimensional unit vectors that uses random rotation followed by optimal scalar quantization per coordinate. It has two variants: TurboQuant_mse (MSE‑optimal) and TurboQuant_prod (unbiased inner product estimation).

For details, see [[concepts/turboquant-overview.md]], [[concepts/turboquant-mse.md]], and [[concepts/turboquant-prod.md]].
