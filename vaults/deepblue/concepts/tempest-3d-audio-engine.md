# Tempest 3D Audio Engine

**Summary**: The Tempest 3D Audio Engine is Sony’s custom audio technology for the PlayStation 5, providing immersive spatial audio. It is designed to deliver 3D sound over headphones and TV speakers.

**Source Context**: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/articles/playstation-5-review-digitalfoundry

---

The Tempest Engine processes audio to create a three‑dimensional soundscape. At launch, 3D audio is available via headphones and is described as additive but not revolutionary compared to a traditional 7.1 surround system. It is one of the key next‑generation features of the [[entities/playstation-5.md]].
