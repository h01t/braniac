# HDMI 2.1, VRR, and 120 Hz on PlayStation 5

**Summary**: PlayStation 5 supports HDMI 2.1 with 120 Hz output in compatible games, but at launch it lacks Variable Refresh Rate (VRR) and Auto Low Latency Mode (ALLM). Native 1440p output is not supported.

**Source Context**: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/articles/playstation-5-review-digitalfoundry

---

The PS5 can output 120 Hz when a game enables it, but VRR is not available at launch. ALLM is also absent. The console does not support native 1440p; 1440p displays receive a downscaled 4K signal. These limitations were noted in the Digital Foundry review of the [[entities/playstation-5.md]].
