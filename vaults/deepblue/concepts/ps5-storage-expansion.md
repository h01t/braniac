# PS5 Storage Expansion

**Summary**: PlayStation 5 includes a slot for PCIe 4.0 M.2 NVMe drives, but it was not functional at launch pending Sony’s certification. External USB drives can store PS4 games but not PS5 titles.

**Source Context**: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/articles/playstation-5-review-digitalfoundry

---

The internal SSD is 825 GB (667 GB usable). The M.2 expansion slot was present on the board but inactive at launch. Sony later enabled it with a firmware update. Backwards‑compatible PS4 games can be played from external USB storage.
