# Prompting Claude Fable 5

**Summary**: Claude Fable 5 responds to standard prompting techniques but has model‑specific guidelines for long‑context prompts and reasoning instructions.
**Source Context**: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/introducing-claude-fable-5-and-claude-mythos-5

---

For detailed prompting strategies, see the [Prompting Claude Fable 5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-fable-5) guide. Key differences from earlier models include how to structure prompts that make effective use of the 1M context window and how to leverage [[concepts/adaptive-thinking.md]] through the [[concepts/effort.md]] parameter.
