# Shannon's Lower Bound (SLB)

**Summary**: A fundamental lower bound in rate-distortion theory that quantifies the minimum achievable distortion for a given bit rate under a source distribution.

**Source Context**: turboqaunt.pdf, Chunk 4 of 5

---

In turboqaunt.pdf, Shannon's lower bound is used as Lemma 3 to bound the MSE for inputs uniformly distributed on the unit sphere. The bound is invoked in the proof of Theorem 3 (lower bound on compression distortion) to show that D_mse ≥ 1/4^b. This aligns with the optimal rate-distortion tradeoff for spherical sources.
