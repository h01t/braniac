# Haptic Feedback

**Summary**: Haptic feedback on the DualSense controller provides nuanced tactile sensations, such as feeling surfaces, wind, and rain through the pad. It is designed to work in tandem with audio to create a cohesive sensory experience.

**Source Context**: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/articles/playstation-5-review-digitalfoundry (DualSense section)

---

The DualSense controller uses advanced haptic actuators to deliver detailed vibrations that simulate real-world textures. In *[[entities/astro-s-playroom.md]]*, the effect is extraordinary, with individual raindrops perceived through the pad. In *[[entities/marvel-s-spider-man-miles-morales.md]]*, haptics are more subtle but natural, especially during web-shooter use.

The haptics are most effective when combined with the controller’s built-in speaker, providing directional and texture-based feedback.
