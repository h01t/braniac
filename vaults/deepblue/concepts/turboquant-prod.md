# TurboQuant Prod (TurboQuant_prod)

**Summary**: TurboQuant_prod is the unbiased inner‑product estimation variant of TurboQuant. It applies the MSE‑optimal quantizer first, then quantizes the residual using Quasi‑Johnson‑Lindenstrauss (QJL) to achieve unbiased estimates.

See [[concepts/quasi-johnson-lindenstrauss-qjl.md]] for the QJL mechanism and [[concepts/turboquant-mse.md]] for the MSE component.
