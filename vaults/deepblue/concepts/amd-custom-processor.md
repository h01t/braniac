# AMD Custom Processor (PlayStation 5)

**Summary**: The PlayStation 5 uses a custom AMD processor with an 8‑core Zen 2 CPU and a custom RDNA 2 GPU. The clock speed fluctuates based on load, defined by a fixed power limit.

**Source Context**: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/articles/playstation-5-review-digitalfoundry

---

The processor’s boost clock varies under thermal and power constraints. Peak system power draw under heavy game load is about 200 W, matching the peak of the [[entities/xbox-series-x.md]].
