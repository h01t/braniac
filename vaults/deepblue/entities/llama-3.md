# Llama 3

**Summary**: Llama 3 is a family of open-source large language models released by Meta. The source references the technical report by Dubey et al. [18].

**Source Context**: turboqaunt.pdf

---

The Llama 3 herd of models ([[18]]) provides a range of scales and serves as a common backbone for quantization experiments, including KV cache compression [[concepts/kv-cache-quantization.md]].
