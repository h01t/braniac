# Marvel’s Spider‑Man: Miles Morales

**Summary**: A launch title for PlayStation 5, used to demonstrate the DualSense controller’s haptic feedback and adaptive triggers. Effects were described as more subtle than *Astro’s Playroom* but natural.

**Source Context**: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/articles/playstation-5-review-digitalfoundry

---

In the review, the game’s haptic feedback is most evident when firing web shooters. It runs at 60 fps on PS5.
