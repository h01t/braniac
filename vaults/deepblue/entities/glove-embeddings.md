# GloVe Embeddings

**Summary**: GloVe (Global Vectors for Word Representation) are standard pre-trained word embeddings. GloVe with dimension 200 is used for near neighbor search experiments.

**Source Context**: turboqaunt.pdf, Chunk 4 of 5

---

In turboqaunt.pdf (Section 4.4), the GloVe dataset (reference [45]) is used as a lower-dimensional dataset (d=200) for [[concepts/near-neighbor-search.md]]. It has a pre-existing query set of 10,000 points. [[concepts/turboquant.md]] is compared against [[concepts/product-quantization.md]] and [[concepts/rabitq.md]] on recall@k.
