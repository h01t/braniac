# PlayStation 5

**Summary**: Sony's ninth-generation console, featuring a custom AMD processor with an 8‑core Zen 2 CPU and RDNA 2 GPU, a custom 825 GB SSD, HDMI 2.1 output, Tempest 3D Audio, and the DualSense controller.

**Source Context**: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/articles/playstation-5-review-digitalfoundry

---

The PS5 is the primary subject of the Digital Foundry review. It includes a custom AMD processor (see [[concepts/amd-custom-processor.md]]), supports HDMI 2.1 features ([[concepts/hdmi-2-1-vrr-and-120hz.md]]), includes an expandable SSD slot ([[concepts/ps5-storage-expansion.md]]), and uses the Tempest 3D Audio Engine ([[concepts/tempest-3d-audio-engine.md]]). The DualSense controller provides haptic feedback ([[concepts/haptic-feedback.md]]).
