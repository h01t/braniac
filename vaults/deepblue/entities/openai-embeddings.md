# OpenAI Embeddings

**Summary**: Embeddings produced by OpenAI's text-embedding-3-large model, used to encode the DBpedia dataset into 1536- and 3072-dimensional spaces.

**Source Context**: turboqaunt.pdf, Chunk 4 of 5

---

The DBpedia Entities dataset is encoded using OpenAI3 embeddings (source: turboqaunt.pdf, Section 4.1 and 4.4). These high-dimensional embeddings are used to validate [[concepts/turboquant.md]]'s theoretical bounds and to benchmark [[concepts/near-neighbor-search.md]].
