# Ministral-7B-Instruct

**Summary**: A 7-billion-parameter instruct-tuned model from Mistral, used in LongBench evaluations of TurboQuant.

**Source Context**: turboqaunt.pdf, Chunk 4 of 5

---

Ministral-7B-Instruct is evaluated on [[concepts/longbench-dataset.md]] in Table 1 of turboqaunt.pdf. TurboQuant at 2.5-bit achieves an average score of 49.62, close to the full cache score of 49.89, outperforming other compression methods.
