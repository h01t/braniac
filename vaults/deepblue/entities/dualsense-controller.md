# DualSense Controller

**Summary**: PlayStation 5’s controller with haptic feedback and adaptive triggers, praised as a revolutionary input device.
**Source Context**: “PlayStation 5 review: welcome to the next generation | Digital Foundry” (https://www.digitalfoundry.net/articles/playstation-5-review-digitalfoundry)

---

The DualSense controller surpasses expectations, offering a premium feel with improved sticks and d-pad. Its key innovations are:

- **Haptic feedback**: Delivers nuanced sensations, such as feeling surfaces, wind, and rain through the pad. In *[[entities/astro-s-playroom.md]]*, the effect is extraordinary, with individual raindrops seemingly felt. In *[[entities/marvel-s-spider-man-miles-morales.md]]*, haptics are more subtle but natural, particularly when firing web shooters.
- **Adaptive triggers**: Provide variable tension, e.g., a spring’s resistance, adding tactile depth to gameplay.

The controller uses its internal speaker in conjunction with haptics to create a cohesive sensory experience. The review notes that Astro’s Playroom was designed around these features, and their impact in other games may be less pronounced. Overall, the DualSense is described as “revolutionary,” though its full potential depends on developer adoption.
