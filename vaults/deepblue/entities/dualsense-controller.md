# DualSense Controller

**Summary**: The PlayStation 5 controller featuring advanced haptic feedback and adaptive triggers. It can simulate surfaces, wind, and rain, and works with the built-in speaker for immersive effects.

**Source Context**: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/articles/playstation-5-review-digitalfoundry

---

The DualSense is described in the Digital Foundry review as providing nuanced tactile sensations. Its haptic effects are demonstrated in [[entities/astro-s-playroom.md]] and [[entities/marvel-s-spider-man-miles-morales.md]].
