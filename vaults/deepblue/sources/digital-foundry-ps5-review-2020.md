# Digital Foundry PS5 Review 2020

**Summary**: A comprehensive technical review of the PlayStation 5 by Richard Leadbetter, published on November 7, 2020.
**Source Context**: Direct article URL (https://www.digitalfoundry.net/articles/playstation-5-review-digitalfoundry)

---

This is the primary source document used to compile all other wiki pages in this set. The article covers:

- Boot‑up and UI (native 4K, HDR, activity cards, performance/quality selector)
- HDMI 2.1 features (120Hz, no VRR, no ALLM, no 1440p, no 8K)
- Hardware analysis (heat, noise, power consumption, thermal imaging)
- DualSense controller and 3D Audio (Tempest Engine)
- Storage and expandability (825GB, M.2 slot not yet supported)
- First impressions of launch titles (*Astro’s Playroom*, *Spider‑Man: Miles Morales*)
- Backwards compatibility successes (60 fps on PS4 Pro games)

The review states that PS5 is “an exceptional console” and “a home run.”
