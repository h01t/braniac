# Claude AI Assistant

**Summary**: The core AI assistant product developed by Anthropic, available in various model sizes and integrated into numerous specialized products.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Product Family
Claude is the flagship AI assistant created by [[entities/anthropic-pbc.md]]. The source material lists its main access points as a standalone product ("Claude"), a downloadable app, and through various integrations.

## Model Variants
Claude is offered in different model sizes and capabilities, including:
*   [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]] (most advanced)
*   Sonnet (balanced)
*   Haiku (fast/compact)
*   Mythos (preview)

## Integrated Products
The Claude AI is the engine behind:
*   [[entities/claude-design.md]]
*   Claude Code
*   Claude Cowork
*   Integrations with Slack, Excel, PowerPoint, Word

## Platform and Ecosystem
Claude is also offered via the Claude Platform API, has a marketplace for plugins, and is available through cloud partners like Amazon Bedrock and Google Vertex AI.
