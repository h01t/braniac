# Claude AI Assistant

**Summary**: The core AI assistant product developed by Anthropic, available in various model sizes and integrated into numerous specialized products.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7.

---

## Product Family
Claude is the flagship AI assistant created by [[entities/anthropic-pbc.md]]. The source material lists its main access points as a standalone product ("Claude"), a downloadable app, and through various integrations (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7).

## Model Variants
Claude is offered in different model sizes and capabilities, including:
*   **[[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]]**: The most advanced model announced in the source article.
*   **Sonnet**: A balanced model.
*   **Haiku**: A fast and compact model.
*   **Mythos**: A model in preview.

## Integrated Products
The Claude AI is the engine behind several specialized products:
*   [[entities/claude-design.md]] for visual work.
*   **Claude Code** for programming.
*   **Claude Cowork** for workspace collaboration.
*   Integrations with tools like Slack, Excel, PowerPoint, and Word.

## Platform and Ecosystem
Claude is also offered via the [[concepts/claude-platform.md]] API, has a marketplace for plugins, and is available through cloud partners like Amazon Bedrock and Google Vertex AI.
