# GDPval-AA Benchmark

**Summary**: A third-party evaluation that measures an AI model's performance on economically valuable knowledge work across domains such as finance and legal.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Description
The GDPval-AA benchmark is developed and hosted by Artificial Analysis. It is designed to assess how well AI models can perform tasks that have direct economic value in professional settings.

## Performance of Claude Opus 4.7
In the announcement for [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]], Anthropic states that the model is "state-of-the-art on GDPval-AA, a third-party evaluation of economically valuable knowledge work across finance, legal, and other domains" (Source: Anthropic News). This claim is used to support the model's capability for real-world professional tasks.
