# Project Glasswing

**Summary**: An Anthropic initiative announced prior to Claude Opus 4.7 that highlights the risks and benefits of AI models for cybersecurity, informing the safety-focused release strategy for powerful models.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Overview
Project Glasswing was announced the week before the release of [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]]. Its purpose is to highlight the dual-use nature of AI in cybersecurity.

## Influence on Model Release Strategy
The project established a policy of keeping the most powerful model, [[entities/claude-mythos-preview.md]], on a limited release while testing new cyber safeguards on less capable models first. Claude Opus 4.7 is described as "the first such model" released under this strategy.

As part of this approach, efforts were made during Opus 4.7's training to "differentially reduce" its cyber capabilities compared to Mythos Preview, and it was launched with automated safeguards to block high-risk cybersecurity requests.
