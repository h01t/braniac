# Prompt Injection

**Summary**: A type of malicious attack where adversarial inputs are crafted to manipulate AI models into performing unintended actions or bypassing safety measures.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Definition and Context
Prompt injection involves inserting harmful prompts to exploit AI vulnerabilities. According to the source, Claude Opus 4.7 shows improved resistance to such attacks compared to Opus 4.6 (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7).

## Role in Safety Evaluations
Resistance to prompt injection is one of the measures where Opus 4.7 is an improvement, contributing to its overall [[concepts/safety-alignment.md]] profile.

## Importance
This concept is critical for ensuring AI systems remain secure and aligned, preventing misuse or data leakage.
