# Software Engineering Capability

**Summary**: Refers to an AI model's proficiency in coding, debugging, and handling complex, long-running software development tasks, a key area of improvement for Claude Opus 4.7.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Definition
Software engineering capability in AI models encompasses the ability to understand, generate, and reason about code, as well as to execute multi-step development workflows autonomously.

## Improvements in Claude Opus 4.7
According to its announcement, [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]] handles "complex, long-running tasks with rigor and consistency" and can hand off the "hardest coding work" (Source: Anthropic News). Testers noted it "catches its own logical faults during the planning phase and accelerates execution" (Source: Anthropic News - Quote).

The model demonstrates strong performance on specific coding benchmarks. For example, it reportedly achieved a 13% lift in resolution on a 93-task coding benchmark over [[entities/claude-opus-4-6.md]] and solved tasks its predecessor could not (Source: Anthropic News - Quote). It also showed a 70% score on CursorBench versus 58% for Opus 4.6 (Source: Anthropic News - Quote).

## Key Behaviors
The model is noted for its autonomy, "more creative reasoning," and ability to work coherently for hours on deep investigation work (Source: Anthropic News - Quote). It resists "looping" indefinitely on queries and shows graceful error recovery (Source: Anthropic News - Quote). A notable behavior is that it "even does proofs on systems code before starting work" (Source: Anthropic News - Quote).
