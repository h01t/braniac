# Next-Generation Compute Partnership

**Summary**: A partnership expansion between Anthropic, Google, and Broadcom to secure multiple gigawatts of next-generation compute infrastructure.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7.

---

## Partnership Details
Anthropic has announced an expanded partnership with technology giants [[entities/google-llc.md]] and [[entities/broadcom-inc.md]] aimed at securing "multiple gigawatts of next-generation compute" (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7). This suggests a large-scale, long-term collaboration to build or secure access to the advanced hardware infrastructure required for developing and deploying large AI models like [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]].

The announcement is framed as an expansion, indicating prior collaborative relationships. The scale ("multiple gigawatts") points to a significant investment in data center capacity and specialized AI chips.

## Strategic Importance
Securing advanced compute is a critical bottleneck and competitive advantage in the AI industry. Partnerships of this scale are essential for supporting the training and inference needs of state-of-the-art models. This move by [[entities/anthropic-pbc.md]] strengthens its infrastructure position against competitors.
