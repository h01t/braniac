# Safety and Alignment

**Summary**: The evaluation and assurance that AI models behave in ways aligned with human values, involving metrics like honesty, resistance to misuse, and low rates of deceptive or harmful behavior.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Definition and Importance
Safety and alignment refer to ensuring AI systems act according to human intentions and ethical standards, preventing behaviors such as deception, sycophancy, and cooperation with misuse. This is critical for trustworthy AI deployment.

## Evaluations in Claude Opus 4.7
According to the announcement, Claude Opus 4.7 has a safety profile similar to Opus 4.6, with improvements in some areas like honesty and resistance to prompt injection attacks, but weaknesses in others such as harm-reduction advice. The model is assessed as "largely well-aligned and trustworthy". [[entities/claude-mythos-preview.md]] is noted as the best-aligned model in Anthropic's evaluations.

## Key Metrics
Metrics include automated behavioral audits scoring misaligned behavior, with Opus 4.7 showing a modest improvement over Opus 4.6. Resistance to [[concepts/prompt-injection.md]] is a specific area of improvement.
