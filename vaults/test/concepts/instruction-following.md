# Instruction Following

**Summary**: The ability of an AI model to accurately interpret and adhere to user-provided directives. Claude Opus 4.7 shows substantially improved instruction following, which can require users to adjust prompts written for earlier, less strict models.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Improvement in Claude Opus 4.7
A highlighted feature of [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]] is that it is "substantially better at following instructions" compared to prior models. The model takes instructions more literally, whereas previous models might interpret them loosely or skip parts.

## Impact on Users
This improvement means that "prompts written for earlier models can sometimes now produce unexpected results." Therefore, Anthropic advises users to "re-tune their prompts and harnesses accordingly" when upgrading to Opus 4.7.

Testers corroborated this, noting the model's "strict instruction following" and its tendency to "push back during technical discussions" rather than simply agreeing with the user.
