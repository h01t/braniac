# Benchmark Evaluations

**Summary**: Standardized tests used to measure and compare the performance of AI models across various domains like coding, reasoning, and professional knowledge work.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Role in Model Assessment
Benchmarks provide quantitative and qualitative measures of an AI model's capabilities. The release of [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]] cites numerous internal and third-party benchmarks to demonstrate its improvements over [[entities/claude-opus-4-6.md]].

## Key Benchmarks Cited
*   **Coding Benchmarks**: The announcement references a 93-task coding benchmark showing a 13% resolution lift, CursorBench (70% vs. 58%), and Rakuten-SWE-Bench (3x more production tasks resolved).
*   **Finance and Agent Benchmarks**: Opus 4.7 is "state-of-the-art" on an internal Finance Agent evaluation and on the third-party [[concepts/gdpval-aa-benchmark.md]] for economically valuable knowledge work.
*   **Specialized Evaluations**: Testers referenced benchmarks like Terminal Bench, TBench, OfficeQA Pro, and BigLaw Bench.
*   **Safety Benchmarks**: Anthropic's automated behavioral audit is used to score "misaligned behavior," showing Opus 4.7 as a modest improvement over Opus 4.6 but not as strong as [[entities/claude-mythos-preview.md]].
