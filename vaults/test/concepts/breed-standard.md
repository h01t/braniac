# Breed Standard

**Summary**: A written document describing the ideal physical traits, movement, and temperament of a specific dog breed, used by breeders and dog show judges.
**Source Context**: https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/

---

Each breed's ideal physical traits, movement, and temperament are set down in a written document called a "breed standard" (per AKC). For example, the breed standard sets forth the traits that make a Cocker Spaniel a Cocker Spaniel and not a Springer Spaniel.

The AKC standard for each breed originates with a "parent club" — the AKC-recognized national club devoted to that particular breed. Once approved by the AKC, a standard becomes both the breeder's "blueprint" and the instrument used by dog show judges to evaluate a breeder's work. The standard ensures that the breed "breeds true" as defined in [[concepts/dog-breed.md]].
