# Claude Opus 4.7

**Summary**: Claude Opus 4.7 is a large language model released by Anthropic in April 2026, representing a notable improvement over its predecessor, Opus 4.6, particularly in advanced software engineering, vision capabilities, and instruction following.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Overview
Claude Opus 4.7 was made generally available on April 16, 2026. It is described as a "notable improvement on Opus 4.6 in advanced software engineering, with particular gains on the most difficult tasks."

## Key Capabilities
The model excels at complex, long-running tasks, paying precise attention to instructions and devising ways to verify its own outputs. Its [[concepts/software-engineering-capability.md]] is a major focus.

It has substantially better vision, accepting higher-resolution images. This [[concepts/multimodal-understanding.md]] enhancement opens up new use cases.

Opus 4.7 is also described as more "tasteful and creative" for professional tasks.

## Performance and Benchmarks
The model shows better results than [[entities/claude-opus-4-6.md]] across a range of benchmarks, though it is noted as being "less broadly capable" than the more powerful [[entities/claude-mythos-preview.md]]. Specific third-party evaluations like [[concepts/gdpval-aa-benchmark.md]] are cited.

## Cybersecurity Context
The release is positioned within the context of [[concepts/project-glasswing.md]], Anthropic's initiative on AI and cybersecurity. Opus 4.7's cyber capabilities are described as not as advanced as Mythos Preview's, as efforts were made during training to "differentially reduce these capabilities". It is released with automated safeguards to detect and block high-risk cyber requests.

## Availability and Pricing
Opus 4.7 is available across all Claude products, the Claude API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud’s Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry. Pricing remains the same as Opus 4.6.

## Safety Profile
According to Anthropic's assessments, Opus 4.7 shows a similar safety profile to Opus 4.6, with low rates of concerning behavior. It shows improvement in honesty and resistance to prompt injection, but is "modestly weaker" in some areas like providing detailed harm-reduction advice.
