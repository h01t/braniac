# Multimodal Understanding

**Summary**: The ability of an AI model to process and interpret information from multiple modalities, such as text and images. Claude Opus 4.7 features improved vision capabilities, including support for higher-resolution images.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Vision Improvements in Claude Opus 4.7
The [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]] model has "substantially better vision" according to its announcement (Source: Anthropic News). It can accept images with a long edge of up to 2,576 pixels (approximately 3.75 megapixels), which is more than three times the capacity of prior Claude models (Source: Anthropic News).

## Applications
This enhanced capability opens up use cases that depend on fine visual detail, such as:
*   Computer-use agents reading dense screenshots.
*   Data extraction from complex diagrams (e.g., chemical structures, technical diagrams).
*   Work requiring pixel-perfect references (Source: Anthropic News).

Testers from Solve Intelligence noted major improvements in "multimodal understanding, from reading chemical structures to interpreting complex technical diagrams" (Source: Anthropic News - Quote). Another tester, XBOW, reported a jump from 54.5% to 98.5% on a visual-acuity benchmark for autonomous penetration testing (Source: Anthropic News - Quote).
