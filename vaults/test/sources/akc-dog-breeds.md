# AKC Dog Breeds Page

**Summary**: The official list of all American Kennel Club dog breeds, with filtering options and an explanation of what constitutes a dog breed.
**Source Context**: https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/

---

This page is the central directory of dog breeds recognized by the American Kennel Club (AKC). It lists over 200 breeds and provides a narrative section titled "What's a dog breed?" that discusses the history and definition of dog breeds.

The page defines a **dog breed** as a group of dogs that always "breeds true" — meaning breeding two purebred individuals of the same breed will produce offspring with predictable traits. Each breed has a written "breed standard" that describes ideal physical traits, movement, and temperament. The standard is created by the breed's "parent club," a national club devoted to that breed, and approved by the AKC.

The page also includes filtering by AKC Group, activity level, barking level, characteristics (e.g., smallest, largest, hypoallergenic), coat type, shedding, size, and trainability. Links to individual breed pages are provided for each recognized breed.
