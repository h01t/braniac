# Partner Testimonials

**Summary**: A collection of quotes and feedback from early-access testing companies that evaluated Claude Opus 4.7 across various domains like coding, finance, legal, and agentic workflows.

## Overview
The announcement for [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]] includes extensive testimonials from over two dozen companies that participated in early testing. These quotes provide qualitative and quantitative evidence of the model's improvements.

## Key Domains and Companies
*   **Financial Technology & Scale**: Quote from a company serving "millions of consumers and businesses" on speed and precision (Source: Anthropic News).
*   **Coding & Software Engineering**: Quotes from companies like Replit, Vercel, and Warp highlighting raw capability, autonomy, and solving previously failed tasks (Source: Anthropic News).
*   **Agentic Workflows & AI Assistants**: Quotes from Notion, Hebbia, and Genspark on reliability, decision-making, and loop resistance in multi-step agent tasks (Source: Anthropic News).
*   **Legal & Professional Services**: Quote from Harvey on substantive accuracy and reasoning on the BigLaw Bench for legal tasks (Source: Anthropic News).
*   **Cybersecurity**: Quote from XBOW on a dramatic improvement in visual acuity for autonomous penetration testing (Source: Anthropic News).
*   **Data Science & Interfaces**: Quotes from Hex on resisting "dissonant-data traps" and a company praising its design taste for dashboards (Source: Anthropic News).
*   **Life Sciences**: Quote from Solve Intelligence on improved multimodal understanding of chemical structures and diagrams (Source: Anthropic News).

## Role in Announcement
These testimonials serve to validate Anthropic's claims about Opus 4.7's capabilities with real-world, third-party evidence from diverse industry applications.
