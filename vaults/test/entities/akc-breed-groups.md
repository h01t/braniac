# AKC Breed Groups

**Summary**: Categories used by the American Kennel Club to organize recognized dog breeds based on general function and history.
**Source Context**: https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/

---

The American Kennel Club ([[entities/american-kennel-club.md]]) divides recognized breeds into the following groups (as listed on the breed directory page):

- **Sporting Group**, **Hound Group**, **Working Group**, **Terrier Group**, **Toy Group**, **Non-Sporting Group**, **Herding Group**, **Miscellaneous Class**, **Foundation Stock Service**

Each group corresponds to historical functions, though many modern dogs serve as companions.
