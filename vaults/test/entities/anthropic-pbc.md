# Anthropic PBC

**Summary**: A public benefit corporation focused on developing safe and reliable AI, known for creating the Claude family of AI models.

## Company Overview
Anthropic PBC is an AI safety and research company. Its core offerings include the [[concepts/claude-ai-assistant.md]] AI assistant, the Claude Platform API, and various enterprise solutions.

## Key Announcements
Recent company news includes:
*   Launching [[entities/claude-design.md]] under its Anthropic Labs division.
*   The [[entities/anthropic-long-term-benefit-trust.md]] appointing Vas Narasimhan to its board.
*   Expanding a [[concepts/next-generation-compute-partnership.md]] with Google and Broadcom.

## Products and Structure
*   **Models**: [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]], Sonnet, Haiku, Mythos (preview).
*   **Core Products**: Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and integrations for Chrome, Slack, and Microsoft Office.
*   **Plans**: Max, Team, and Enterprise pricing plans.
*   **Platform**: A developer platform with API, marketplace, and partnerships with Amazon Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry.
*   **Governance**: Notable for its Responsible Scaling Policy.
