# Claude Mythos Preview

**Summary**: A more powerful and capable AI model developed by Anthropic, kept on a limited release as part of the Project Glasswing safety strategy. It serves as a benchmark for the upper limits of capability and alignment.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Capability Context
[[entities/claude-mythos-preview.md]] is described as Anthropic's "most powerful model" (Source: Anthropic News). It is used as a point of comparison to illustrate that [[concepts/claude-opus-4-7.md]], while improved, is "less broadly capable" than Mythos Preview (Source: Anthropic News).

## Release Strategy and Safety
Following the announcement of [[concepts/project-glasswing.md]], Anthropic stated it would "keep Claude Mythos Preview’s release limited and test new cyber safeguards on less capable models first" (Source: Anthropic News). Opus 4.7 is the first model released under this policy.

Mythos Preview is also noted as having more advanced cyber capabilities than Opus 4.7, and as being the "best-aligned model we’ve trained according to our evaluations," showing the lowest rates of misaligned behavior in Anthropic's audit (Source: Anthropic News).
