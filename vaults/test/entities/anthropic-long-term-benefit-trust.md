# Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust

## Board Appointment
The Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust has appointed **Vas Narasimhan** to its Board of Directors. Narasimhan is the CEO of Novartis AG.

## Purpose and Context
A "Long-Term Benefit Trust" is a governance structure sometimes used by companies, like [[entities/anthropic-pbc.md]], to align corporate decision-making with long-term mission and safety goals.
