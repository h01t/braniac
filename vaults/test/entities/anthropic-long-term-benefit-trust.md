# Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust

## Board Appointment
The Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust has appointed **Vas Narasimhan** to its Board of Directors (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7). Narasimhan is the CEO of [[entities/novartis-ag.md]].

## Purpose and Context
A "Long-Term Benefit Trust" is a governance structure sometimes used by companies, like [[entities/anthropic-pbc.md]], to align corporate decision-making with long-term mission and safety goals, often distinct from short-term shareholder interests. This appointment indicates Anthropic is adding experienced external leadership from outside the tech industry to this key oversight body.
