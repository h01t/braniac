# American Kennel Club (AKC)

**Summary**: A not-for-profit organization founded in 1884, recognized as the expert in breed, health, and training information for all dogs. It maintains the official list of dog breeds and approves breed standards.
**Source Context**: https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/

---

The American Kennel Club (AKC) is the recognized and trusted expert in breed, health, and training information for all dogs. It actively advocates for responsible dog ownership and is dedicated to advancing dog sports. The AKC was founded in 1884.

The AKC maintains the official list of [[concepts/dog-breed.md]] and recognizes 200 breeds (out of over 340 known worldwide). Each breed's [[concepts/breed-standard.md]] originates with a "parent club" and must be approved by the AKC. The AKC also categorizes breeds into groups (see [[entities/akc-breed-groups.md]]).
