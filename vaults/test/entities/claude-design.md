# Claude Design

**Summary**: A new product from Anthropic Labs that enables collaborative creation of visual designs, prototypes, slides, and one-pagers with the Claude AI.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7.

---

## Product Launch
Claude Design was launched as a new product under **Anthropic Labs** (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7). It is designed for visual work, allowing users to collaborate with the [[concepts/claude-ai-assistant.md]] to create polished outputs.

## Capabilities
The product is intended for creating a range of visual materials, including:
*   Designs
*   Prototypes
*   Slides
*   One-pagers

This positions Claude Design as a tool for professionals in design, marketing, and business who need to generate visual content rapidly. It represents an expansion of Claude's capabilities beyond text and code into the visual design domain.
