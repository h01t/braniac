# Claude Design

**Summary**: A new product from Anthropic Labs that enables collaborative creation of visual designs, prototypes, slides, and one-pagers with the Claude AI.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

---

## Product Launch
Claude Design was launched as a new product under **Anthropic Labs**. It is designed for visual work, allowing users to collaborate with the [[concepts/claude-ai-assistant.md]] to create polished outputs.

## Capabilities
The product is intended for creating a range of visual materials, including designs, prototypes, slides, and one-pagers.
