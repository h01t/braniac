## Alignment Status
According to the announcement, Mythos Preview has the lowest rates of misaligned behavior in Anthropic's automated behavioral audits, making it the best-aligned model they've trained (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7).

## Comparison with Opus 4.7
Opus 4.7 is noted to be modestly weaker than Mythos Preview in alignment, but still shows improvements over Opus 4.6 (Source: same). This highlights the ongoing efforts in [[concepts/safety-alignment.md]].

## Context
Mythos Preview is referenced as a benchmark for alignment excellence within Anthropic's model lineup.
