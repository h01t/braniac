# Multi-Task Language Understanding

**Summary**: This refers to evaluating LLMs on a broad range of diverse language tasks within a single benchmark to measure general capability.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf, Table 12.

---

## Benchmarks and Performance
Key benchmarks for this capability include:
*   **BIG-bench**: A large, collaborative benchmark of diverse tasks. Top performers included Chinchilla (70B), Gopher (280B), and PaLM (540B) in a 5-shot setting.
*   **MMLU (Massive Multitask Language Understanding)**: A benchmark covering 57 subjects. Top performers include GPT-4, Gemini Ultra, and Flan-PaLM-2 (Large) in a 5-shot setting [Source, Table 12].

These benchmarks test a model's ability to generalize across different types of questions without task-specific fine-tuning.
