# DeepSeek-67B-Chat
A 67 billion parameter chat model variant of the **[[entities/deepseek_llm.md]]** series.

**Notable Characteristics:**
*   Exhibited a notable discrepancy in intelligence across tasks compared to smaller models when scaled.
*   Demonstrated strong safety performance across a manually constructed taxonomy of 2400 test questions.
*   Achieved a high score (97.8) on the **[[sources/do_not_answer_dataset.md|Do-Not-Answer Dataset]]**, surpassing ChatGPT and GPT-4 at the time of evaluation.
*   Performance significantly improved with the addition of a **[[concepts/system_prompt_impact.md|system prompt]]**.
