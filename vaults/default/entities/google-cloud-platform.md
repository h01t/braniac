# Google Cloud Platform

**Summary**: Google Cloud Platform (GCP) is Google's suite of cloud computing services, including computing, storage, and AI tools, which aids enterprise digital transformation and contributes to productivity gains through scalable infrastructure.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

Google Cloud Platform is a key part of Google's enterprise strategy, competing with AWS and Microsoft Azure.

## Services and Economic Role
Expanding since 2008, GCP provides infrastructure (IaaS), platforms (PaaS), and software (SaaS) that help businesses shift from on-premises setups to the cloud, lowering capital costs. It is integral to Google's [[concepts/digital-economy-contributions.md|contributions to the digital economy]], enabling scalable computing for businesses of all sizes.

## AI and Productivity Integration
GCP hosts advanced AI services like Vertex AI. The source notes that Vertex AI yielded a 397% return on investment for enterprises over three years by accelerating AI model development and deployment. GCP is also the platform for delivering [[entities/gemini-ai.md|Gemini AI]] capabilities to business customers.

## Broader Impact
The platform's tools are part of the productivity ecosystem that Google estimates contributed $850 billion to U.S. economic activity in 2024. It also supports the backend infrastructure for many other Google services, including [[entities/google-workspace.md|Google Workspace]] and YouTube.
