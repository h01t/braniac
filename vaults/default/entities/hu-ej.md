# E. J. Hu

**Summary**: The lead author of the seminal LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation) paper, which introduced a highly influential parameter-efficient fine-tuning method for large language models.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

E. J. Hu is the first author of "LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models" (2021) [[sources/hu-et-al-lora-2021.md]]. This work introduced [[concepts/lora-low-rank-adaptation.md]], a cornerstone technique in [[concepts/training-efficiency-techniques.md]] that has been widely adopted in both academia and industry for adapting LLMs.
