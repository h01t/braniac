# SDAIA-KFUPM Joint Research Center for AI

**Summary**: A collaborative research center between the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) and King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM), providing grant funding for AI research.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Overview
The Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) and King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM) jointly operate a research center dedicated to Artificial Intelligence. This entity provided grant funding (Grant No. JRC-AI-RFP-11) supporting the work presented in the "Comprehensive Overview of LLMs" source material.

## Role in LLM Research
The center's support, acknowledged in the source, facilitated the creation of a survey/resource document intended to advance LLM research. This indicates institutional investment in foundational AI research within the region, specifically targeting the development of large language models [[concepts/llm-development-goals.md]].
