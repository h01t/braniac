# OpenAI-o1-mini

**Summary**: A reasoning-optimized language model developed by OpenAI, used as a performance benchmark in the DeepSeek-R1 research.
**Source Context**: DeepSeek_R1.pdf

---

## Context in the DeepSeek-R1 Paper
**OpenAI-o1-mini** is referenced in the DeepSeek-R1 paper as one of the comparable models against which the DeepSeek models are evaluated. It represents a state-of-the-art reasoning model from a leading AI lab.

## Performance Comparison
The paper presents benchmark results (Table 5) comparing various models. Key comparisons involving o1-mini include:
*   The larger distilled models from the DeepSeek-R1 family, specifically **DeepSeek-R1-32B and DeepSeek-R1-70B** (referring to DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-70B), are noted to **"significantly exceed o1-mini on most benchmarks"** (Source: DeepSeek_R1.pdf).
*   The overall performance target for the flagship [[entities/deepseek-r1.md]] teacher model is stated to be **"comparable to OpenAI-o1-1217"** on a range of tasks (Source: DeepSeek_R1.pdf).

These comparisons position the DeepSeek-R1 models as competitive with or superior to OpenAI's reasoning-optimized offerings in specific evaluations.
