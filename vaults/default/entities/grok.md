# Grok

**Summary**: A family of large language models released by xAI, including the large MoE model Grok-1 and the multimodal Grok-1.5.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Model Variants
The Grok family includes at least two notable models:
*   **Grok-1**: A 314-billion parameter language model built on a [[concepts/mixture-of-experts.md]] (MoE) architecture. It uses 8 experts, with 2 experts activated per token.
*   **Grok-1.5**: A multi-modal large language model (MLLM) with improvements over Grok-1, including a larger context length and better performance.
