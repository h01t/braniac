# Expert Safety Team
A multidisciplinary team of approximately 20 experts assembled to ensure the safety of **[[entities/deepseek_llm.md]]**.

**Responsibilities:**
1.  Constructed a safety content classification system (taxonomy) aligned with human values.
2.  Manually created dozens of high-quality test cases for each safety subcategory, ensuring diversity in both content and query format (e.g., inducement, role-playing).
3.  Established basic guideline constitutions for safety reviews for different content and format types.
4.  Performed manual safety inspection and annotation (safe/unsafe/refusal) on model outputs, with cross-verification among annotators.
