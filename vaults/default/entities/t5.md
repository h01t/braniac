# T5

**Summary**: An encoder-decoder model that uses a unified text-to-text training approach for all NLP problems.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf, Batch 2 of 15.

---

T5 is a pre-trained LLM with key features:

- **Architecture**: Based on an encoder-decoder transformer with layer normalization outside the residual path, as shown in Figure 7 of the source [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf].

- **Training Objective**: Employs masked language modeling where spans of consecutive tokens are replaced with a single mask, speeding up training [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf].

- **Fine-Tuning**: Uses adapter layers for downstream tasks after pre-training [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf].

T5 demonstrates the effectiveness of [[concepts/training-objectives.md]] and is part of the [[concepts/adaptation-stages.md]] pipeline.
