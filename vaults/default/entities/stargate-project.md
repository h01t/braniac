# Stargate Project

**Summary**: A large-scale AI data center infrastructure joint venture between OpenAI, Oracle, and SoftBank, aiming for massive compute capacity expansion.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Project Overview
In 2025, OpenAI accelerated infrastructure expansion via the Stargate project, a joint venture with [[entities/oracle.md]] and [[entities/softbank.md]] targeting up to 10 gigawatts of capacity by year-end, backed by a $500 billion commitment [[sources/ref-54.md]].
The project progressed ahead of schedule, announcing five additional sites on September 23, 2025, including a $15 billion "Lighthouse" campus in Port Washington, Wisconsin, with [[entities/oracle.md]] and Vantage Data Centers [[sources/ref-54-55.md]].

## Capacity and Partnerships
OpenAI also partnered with [[entities/oracle.md]] for up to 4.5 gigawatts of U.S.-based Stargate capacity in July 2025, committing $300 billion over five years to Oracle's infrastructure [[sources/ref-56-57.md]].
To support the buildout, OpenAI formed hardware partnerships, including agreements with [[entities/nvidia.md]], [[entities/amd.md]], and [[entities/broadcom.md]] [[sources/ref-58-62.md]].

## Community and Environmental Plan
On January 20, 2026, OpenAI unveiled the Stargate Community plan, committing to fund dedicated power generation, grid upgrades, and cooling innovations to minimize environmental impact and avoid raising local electricity costs [[concepts/infrastructure-community-plan.md]].
