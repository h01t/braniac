# Nemotron-4 340B

**Summary**: A 340-billion parameter decoder-only model aligned almost entirely (98%) on synthetic data, using an iterative training process to create strong instruction-following models.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Training and Alignment Strategy
Nemotron-4 340B's key innovation is its heavy reliance on **synthetic data for alignment** [[concepts/synthetic-data-alignment.md]]. The model was aligned using a dataset where 98% of the data was synthetically generated by other models, and only 2% was manually annotated by humans. This approach was found to significantly improve performance.

The training process involved an iterative bootstrapping method:
1.  Weaker instruction-tuned models generate synthetic instruction-response data.
2.  This synthetic data is used to train a base model.
3.  The base model is then supervised fine-tuned, resulting in a model that outperforms the weaker model that generated the data.

To ensure a smooth transition, the paper suggested introducing the alignment data alongside a small subset of previously seen pre-training data during the late stages of the model's overall training.
