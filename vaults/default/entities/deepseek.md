# DeepSeek

**Summary**: DeepSeek is a Hangzhou-based AI startup known for releasing high-efficiency, open-source [[concepts/large-language-model.md]]s built on [[concepts/mixture-of-experts.md]] (MoE) architectures, emphasizing low running costs and strong performance in reasoning and coding benchmarks.
**Source Context**: https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/chinese-open-source-llms-2025

---

## Key Model Releases
DeepSeek burst onto the scene with its MoE-based models:
*   **DeepSeek V3**: Open-sourced in late 2024 on Hugging Face, estimated at ~250B parameters with only 37B active per query [^18].
*   **DeepSeek R1**: A reasoning-optimized model open-sourced in February 2025, along with its code repositories for full transparency [^19].
*   **DeepSeek V3.2-Exp**: An experimental intermediate version published on Hugging Face in September 2025 [^20].

## Technical and Strategic Focus
The company emphasizes low operational costs and has released its Native Sparse Attention algorithm alongside these models [^21][^22]. Industry reports rank DeepSeek’s models at the top of domestic Chinese benchmarks for reasoning and coding [^21]. Its open-source strategy involves sharing both model weights and code, "doubling down" on transparency [^19].

## Competitive Context
DeepSeek's models are central competitors within the [[concepts/chinese-open-source-llm-landscape-2025.md]], often cited in performance comparisons by other firms like [[entities/alibaba-cloud.md]] [^7].
