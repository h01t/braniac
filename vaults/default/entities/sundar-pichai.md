# Sundar Pichai

**Summary**: Sundar Pichai is the CEO of both Google LLC and its parent company, Alphabet Inc., having risen through the ranks after leading the development of products like the Chrome browser.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

Sundar Pichai joined [[entities/google-llc.md|Google]] in 2004 and gained prominence for managing the development and launch of the Chrome browser and Chrome OS. His leadership of key product areas led to his appointment as Google's CEO in 2015 and later as CEO of [[entities/alphabet-inc.md]] in 2019 (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).

## Leadership Tenure
As CEO, Pichai has overseen Google's continued dominance in search and advertising, its expansion into cloud computing and hardware, and the integration of advanced [[concepts/artificial-intelligence.md]] across its products. He navigates significant regulatory challenges and market competition.
