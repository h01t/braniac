# Sora

**Summary**: OpenAI's text-to-video generative model capable of creating short, high-definition video clips, which was later discontinued to reallocate compute resources toward other priorities.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Release and Features

Sora debuted on February 15, 2024, as a [[concepts/text-to-video-generation.md]] model. It could generate 60-second 1080p video clips with complex motions, characters, and interactions while adhering to user prompts [186].

Public access began on December 9, 2024, via sora.com with initial 20-second limits, alongside features for remixing, looping, and creating storyboards. Sora 2 was released on September 30, 2025, enhancing physical accuracy, realism, and control, and adding audio generation. Extensions like pet videos and sharing features followed in October 2025 [187, 188].

## Capabilities and Limitations

Sora excels at generating dynamic scenes, such as a woolly mammoth in snow or a fantasy astronaut. However, it struggles with accurately depicting human interactions, maintaining consistency, and rendering rare events due to limitations in its training data [189].

## Safety and Copyright

To curb potential misuse, OpenAI mandated copyright opt-outs for training data and deployed deepfake detection safeguards. This policy sparked debates about intellectual property in the field of [[concepts/video-synthesis.md]] [190].

## Discontinuation (2026)

In late March 2026, OpenAI discontinued the standalone Sora application. Reporting by *The Information* revealed this decision was driven by the need to reallocate compute resources (GPUs and engineering focus) away from the resource-intensive video product. The shift was part of a strategic pivot toward prioritizing core growth areas like enterprise tools and the development of a new frontier model internally codenamed "Spud" [Source Context].
