# Open-weight Models

**Summary**: OpenAI's strategic release of openly licensed, smaller-scale language models in 2025, marking a shift to compete in the open-source ecosystem.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Release and Models

In a shift from its historically closed-source stance, OpenAI entered the open-weight ecosystem in 2025. On August 5, 2025, it released two models: **gpt-oss-120b** and **gpt-oss-20b**. These were the company's first such releases since 2019 [219].

These open-weight language models are optimized for cost-effective performance in real-world applications and are made available through partnerships with deployment providers [219, 220].

## Strategic Context

This move was a strategic adaptation to competitive pressures from companies like [[entities/companies/meta.md]] and [[entities/companies/mistral-ai.md]] that were releasing open models. It was previewed in announcements made in April 2025 [221].

The release prioritized accessibility for developers while maintaining safeguards against misuse. However, these models trail OpenAI's proprietary, frontier models (like the `o3` series) in terms of scale and benchmark performance [220, 221].
