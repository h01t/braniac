# LLaMA2
A family of large language models developed by Meta, used as a key baseline for comparison.

**Context in DeepSeek LLM Report:**
*   **Comparison Point:** DeepSeek models are compared against LLaMA2 7B and 70B.
*   **Pre-training:** LLaMA2 models consumed 2T tokens but focused on English, while DeepSeek used a 2T bilingual corpus.
*   **Performance:** Despite no specific Chinese training, LLaMA2 shows impressive performance on some Chinese tasks like [[entities/cmath.md]], demonstrating cross-language transfer of fundamental abilities like math reasoning.
*   **Language Conflict:** The performance gap analysis suggests language conflict in bilingual training affects smaller models more.
