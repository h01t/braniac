# Alphabet Financial Performance

**Summary**: Alphabet Inc., Google's parent, generates most revenue from advertising, with consistent growth in revenue and net income, reaching a market capitalization of $3.88 trillion in early 2026.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

## Revenue Streams and Growth
Advertising, primarily via Google Search and YouTube, comprised about 75% of Alphabet's total revenue in 2024 (Source ref: 143). Consolidated revenue for 2024 was $350.02 billion, a 13.9% increase from 2023's $307.39 billion, driven by search ads and Google Cloud growth (Source ref: 144). Net income saw a significant 35.7% rise to $100.12 billion in 2024 (Source ref: 145). In Q2 2025, revenue was $96.4 billion, a 14% year-over-year increase (Source ref: 146).

## Market Valuation
In early January 2026, Alphabet's market capitalization reached $3.88 trillion, briefly exceeding Apple's to become the world's second most valuable company behind Nvidia (Source ref: 148). This followed a 65% stock rally in 2025, fueled by AI advances like the Ironwood chip and Gemini 3, and strong Google Cloud demand (Source ref: 148). Valuation metrics showed a trailing P/E of 26.27, reflecting a growth premium (Source ref: 149).

## Capital Expenditure
Projected capital expenditures for 2025 were approximately $85 billion, primarily allocated to data centers and AI infrastructure (Source ref: 150).
