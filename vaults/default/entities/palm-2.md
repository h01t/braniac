# PaLM-2

**Summary**: A large language model from Google, frequently appearing as a top performer (specifically the "Large" variant) across various language understanding and reasoning benchmarks.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf, Table 12.

---

## Model Overview
The "PaLM-2 (Large)" variant is highlighted in performance comparisons. It is an evolution of the original PaLM model.

## Benchmark Performance
PaLM-2 (Large) achieved top-tier results on multiple benchmarks, often in "one-shot" settings [Source, Table 12]:
*   **HellaSwag**: 86.8% (one-shot)
*   **StoryCloze**: 87.4% (one-shot)
*   **PIQA**: 85.0% (one-shot)
*   **TriviaQA**: 86.1% (one-shot)
*   **LAMBADA**: 86.9% (one-shot)
*   **BoolQ**: 90.9% (one-shot)
*   **GSM8K**: 80.7% (8-shot)
*   **MATH**: 34.3% (4-shot)
