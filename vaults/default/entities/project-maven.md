# Project Maven

**Summary**: A U.S. Department of Defense initiative that used artificial intelligence to analyze drone footage, which sparked significant internal protest at Google over ethical concerns about military use of AI.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

Project Maven, also known as the Algorithmic Warfare Cross-Functional Team, was a Pentagon program launched in 2017. Its goal was to use machine learning and AI to automatically identify objects of interest in vast amounts of drone surveillance footage.

**Google's Involvement and Employee Backlash**
Google participated in Project Maven, providing AI expertise and technology. This involvement triggered a major internal crisis in early 2018. Over 3,100 Google employees signed a petition demanding the company end its work on the project, and dozens resigned in protest.

Employees raised [[concepts/ethical-ai.md]] concerns, arguing that building AI for military surveillance and potential targeting conflicted with Google's former "Don't be evil" motto and could pave the way for autonomous weapons. The protest was a key moment in tech worker [[concepts/labor-organizing.md]].

**Google's Response**
Facing intense pressure, Google announced in June 2018 that it would not renew its contract for Project Maven when it expired. The company also published a set of "AI Principles," which included a pledge not to design or deploy AI for weapons or technologies that cause injury. However, Google stated it would continue other collaborations with the military in areas like cybersecurity, search and rescue, and veterans' healthcare.
