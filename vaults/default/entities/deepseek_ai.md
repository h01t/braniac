# DeepSeek-AI
DeepSeek-AI is the organization behind the [[entities/deepseek_llm.md]] project. It focuses on advancing open-source language models with a commitment to long-term development.

**Authors:**
- Listed alphabetically in the paper, including Xiao Bi, Deli Chen, and others.
- The team contributed to data collection, model training, and evaluation.

The project aims to address scaling challenges and provide reference for the community, as detailed in [[sources/deepseek_llm_paper.md]].
