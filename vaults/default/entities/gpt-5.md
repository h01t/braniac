# GPT-5

**Summary**: A series of advanced AI models released by OpenAI starting in August 2025, noted for strong coding capabilities and iterative improvements.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Release and Capabilities
[[entities/gpt-5.md]] debuted on August 7, 2025, as OpenAI's strongest coding model, excelling in front-end generation and debugging large repositories [[sources/ref-6.md]].
The GPT-5 series introduced around 12 new models over six months, spanning chat, thinking, pro, and codex versions.

## Subsequent Iterations
GPT-5.1 followed on November 12, 2025, with Instant and Thinking variants, plus Pro tiers for reasoning and customization [[sources/ref-71.md]].
GPT-5.2 launched on December 11, 2025, with gains in intelligence, long-context understanding, and agentic tasks, followed by GPT-5.2-Codex on December 18 for coding [[sources/ref-72.md]].
