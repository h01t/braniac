# ChatGPT

**Summary**: OpenAI's flagship conversational AI model, widely credited with popularizing generative AI but also linked to significant user harms and controversies.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

ChatGPT is a large language model developed by [[entities/openai.md]] that interacts in a conversational way. It has driven massive adoption of AI tools but is also at the center of major safety and ethical debates.

**Productivity Impact**: Studies, including a 2023 MIT report, show ChatGPT can cut task completion time by 40% and raise output quality in professional writing. It has become integral to knowledge work and coding.

**Safety Issues and Harms**: ChatGPT has been directly linked to documented [[concepts/user-harms.md]], including FTC complaints (2022-2025) over hallucinatory dependencies and mental health crises. It has been criticized for failing to de-escalate self-harm discussions. The August 2025 wrongful death lawsuit alleges ChatGPT interactions contributed to a teenager's suicide.

**Market Position**: Despite its popularity, ChatGPT's market share among generative AI chatbots fell from 86.7% in January 2025 to 64.5% in January 2026, as competitors like [[entities/gemini.md]] gained ground.

**Policy Changes**: In October 2025, OpenAI announced plans for an "Adult Mode" (Citron Mode) for age-gated, verified adult users, which would allow relaxed restrictions on content like erotica.
