# ChatGPT

**Summary**: ChatGPT is an AI-powered conversational agent developed by OpenAI, noted in the source as a competitive challenger to Google's dominance in search and information retrieval, pressuring Google to accelerate its own AI integrations.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

ChatGPT represents a paradigm shift in human-computer interaction, providing a conversational interface for information tasks that competes with traditional web search.

## Competitive Pressure on Google
The source identifies AI challengers like ChatGPT as exerting significant pressure on Google's core search business. The ability of ChatGPT to provide direct, conversational answers challenges the traditional list-of-links search model. This competitive dynamic is cited as a factor in Google's rapid integration of its own [[entities/gemini-ai.md|Gemini AI]] features into Google Search to provide similar AI overviews and summaries.

## Market Context
Despite the hype and user adoption of ChatGPT, the source notes that Google's search market share remains extremely high (over 90% as of late 2025), and network effects have limited large-scale user shifts. However, ChatGPT's rise is used both by regulators to argue markets are dynamic and by Google's defense to counter claims of an unassailable monopoly.
