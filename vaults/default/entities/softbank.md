# SoftBank

**Summary**: A Japanese multinational investment holding company and a major investor in OpenAI, with cumulative investments reaching $64.6 billion by early 2026 through preferred shares convertible upon an IPO.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Investments in OpenAI
SoftBank's involvement with [[entities/openai.md]] escalated significantly in 2025 and 2026:
*   **December 2025**: Completed a $41 billion investment commitment ($7.5 billion initial in April 2025, plus $22.5 billion additional in late December). This gave SoftBank approximately 11% ownership at the time.
*   **February 27, 2026**: Announced a $30 billion follow-on investment as part of OpenAI's $110 billion funding round. This investment was divided into three tranches of $10 billion each, planned to close in April, July, and October 2026.

## Strategic Positioning & Outcome
This $30 billion investment brought SoftBank's total stake in OpenAI to approximately 13% and its cumulative investment to $64.6 billion via preferred shares convertible to common shares upon an IPO (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI). The investment advances SoftBank's artificial superintelligence strategy and supports OpenAI's growth. SoftBank reported significant gains on its stake in early 2026 financials.
