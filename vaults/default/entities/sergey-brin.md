# Sergey Brin

**Summary**: Sergey Brin is an American computer scientist and entrepreneur who co-founded Google with Larry Page. He has held key leadership roles, including President of Alphabet Inc.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

Sergey Brin collaborated with [[entities/larry-page.md]] at Stanford University to create the [[concepts/pagerank.md]] algorithm and co-found [[entities/google-llc.md|Google]] in 1998. His contributions were critical in shaping the company's early technology and culture.

## Role at Google and Alphabet
Brin served as President of Technology and later as President of [[entities/alphabet-inc.md]] following its formation in 2015. Alongside Page, he was instrumental in guiding Google's strategic direction and fostering innovation in areas like [[concepts/artificial-intelligence.md]] and emerging technologies.

## Innovations and Legacy
Brin's expertise in data mining and extracting meaning from large datasets complemented Page's work on web linking, leading to the creation of a superior search engine. He has been actively involved in special projects, including Google's early forays into wearable technology and self-driving cars.
