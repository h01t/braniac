# Habtom Kidane Gidey

**Summary**: A researcher whose work spans formal methods, software architecture, cognitive bots, and the application of grounded theory to AI and agent design.
**Source Context**: Fundamentals of Building Autonomous LLM Agents.pdf (References).

---

## Research Focus and Contributions
Habtom Kidane Gidey's research intersects software engineering and artificial intelligence. Key themes include:
*   **Formal Methods and Architecture**: Co-developed **FACTum Studio**, a tool for modeling and verifying dynamic software architectures using formal methods (Gidey & Marmsoler, 2019; 2018). Applied grounded theory to software architecture design (Gidey et al., 2017).
*   **Cognitive Bots and Automation**: Authored surveys and research on "user-like bots" and architectural challenges for **cognitive automation** (Gidey et al., 2023 x2), exploring how bots can emulate human cognitive processes.
*   **Agents and Affordances**: Recent work investigates **affordance representation and recognition** for autonomous agents (Gidey et al., 2025) and **visual perception patterns** for software agents (Gidey et al., 2025 preprint).
*   **Knowledge and Adaptive Systems**: Explored document-based knowledge discovery with microservices (Gidey et al., 2022) and modeled adaptive self-healing systems (Gidey et al., 2023).

## Connection to Autonomous LLM Agents
Gidey's work provides a rigorous, formal, and human-centered perspective on building intelligent systems. The research on **cognitive bots** directly informs the design of [[concepts/autonomous-llm-agents.md]] that can interact naturally and reliably. The focus on **affordances** and **visual perception** is relevant for [[concepts/multimodal-llms.md]] operating in real environments. The architectural work emphasizes the need for verifiable and well-founded agent designs.
