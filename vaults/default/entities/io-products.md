# io Products, Inc.

**Summary**: An AI hardware startup founded by former Apple design chief Jony Ive, acquired by OpenAI in May 2025.

## Acquisition by OpenAI
In May 2025, OpenAI acquired io Products, Inc. in a deal valued at approximately $6.4 billion [[sources/ref-52-53.md]]. The acquisition was aimed at integrating advanced product design expertise into OpenAI's operations.
The company was founded by former [[entities/apple.md]] design chief [[entities/jony-ive.md]].
