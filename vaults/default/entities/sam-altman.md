# Sam Altman

**Summary**: Sam Altman is the co-founder and CEO of OpenAI, a central figure in the AI industry who was briefly ousted and reinstated in a 2023 governance crisis. He is a prominent advocate for AI development and its societal impact.
**Source Context**: Axios timeline of OpenAI chaos (https://www.axios.com/2023/11/22/openai-microsoft-sam-altman-ceo-chaos-timeline), TIME 100 AI profile (https://time.com/collections/time100-ai-2025/7305828/sam-altman-ai/).

---

## Role at OpenAI
Sam Altman co-founded OpenAI in 2015 and has served as its CEO, guiding the company from a non-profit research lab to a dominant, multi-billion dollar AI enterprise. He was a key architect of its transition to a [[concepts/capped-profit-structure.md]] and its landmark partnership with [[entities/microsoft.md]].

## 2023 Leadership Crisis
In November 2023, the OpenAI board fired Altman as CEO, citing a lack of candor. This triggered a major crisis, with most of OpenAI's staff threatening to quit. He was reinstated as CEO just five days later after a deal was struck that reconstituted the board (Source: Axios, New York Times). A later investigation concluded that his conduct did not mandate removal (Source: OpenAI).

## Public Advocacy and Views
Altman is a frequent public commentator on AI. He has testified before Congress, warned about the potential risks of superintelligent AI, and expressed concerns that the US underestimates [[concepts/sovereign-ai.md]] capabilities in [[entities/china.md]] (Source: CNBC, Time). He has also stated that the current AI market is in a bubble (Source: CNBC, Fortune) and that export controls alone won't hold back China's AI ambitions (Source: Tom's Hardware).

## Other Ventures and Influence
Beyond OpenAI, Altman has been involved in other ventures, including the startup studio Hydrazine Capital and a focus on nuclear fusion through Helion Energy. He is consistently ranked among the most influential people in AI (Source: TIME).
