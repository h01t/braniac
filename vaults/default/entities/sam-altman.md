# Sam Altman

**Summary**: Co-founder and CEO of OpenAI, a key figure in its leadership, strategy, and fundraising efforts.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Role at OpenAI
[[entities/sam-altman.md]] has served as CEO of OpenAI since 2019, following his role as president of the initial nonprofit [[sources/ref-77.md]]. He was a member of the founding team in December 2015 [[sources/ref-76.md]].
He was briefly ousted and reinstated in November 2023 after a board vote citing lack of candor [[sources/ref-77-78.md]].

## Recent Activities
In late March 2026, Altman informed employees that the company had completed pre-training on its next major AI model, codenamed "Spud," which he described as a "very strong model" [[concepts/frontier-model-development.md]].
Concurrently, he restructured his direct reports, relinquishing oversight of safety and security teams to focus on capital raising, supply chains, and large-scale data center construction.
