# Sam Altman

**Summary**: Co-founder and CEO of OpenAI, a central figure in the company's strategic direction, public advocacy, and controversies.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

Sam Altman is the CEO of [[entities/openai.md]] and a prominent voice in the AI industry. His leadership and public statements have been influential and frequently controversial.

**Regulatory Stance**: In 2025, Altman warned that strict regulations like the [[entities/eu-ai-act.md]] might limit European access to AI, benefiting less-regulated regions. He has defended OpenAI's policy moves, including a planned "Adult Mode" for ChatGPT, stating the company was not the "world's moral police."

**Controversies and Lawsuits**: Altman was named as a defendant in the August 2025 wrongful death lawsuit related to a teenager's suicide after interactions with [[entities/chatgpt.md]]. Following the rushed 2026 Department of Defense agreement, Altman admitted internally and publicly that the deal "looked opportunistic and sloppy" and that OpenAI "shouldn't have rushed" the announcement.

**Predictions and Hype**: Altman has made bold predictions about achieving [[concepts/agi.md]] by 2025 and superintelligence by 2030, which critics cite as examples of overhyping AI capabilities.
