# Sam Altman

**Summary**: American entrepreneur and investor, co-founder and CEO of OpenAI, and former president of the startup accelerator Y Combinator.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

**Sam Altman** is a co-founder and the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of [[entities/openai.md]]. He was also a co-chair of the organization at its founding in December 2015. Prior to OpenAI, Altman was the president of the startup accelerator [[entities/y-combinator.md]] (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

Altman is a central figure in OpenAI's history and strategy. He was instrumental in the organization's shift from a pure non-profit to a [[concepts/capped-profit-structure.md]] in 2019 to secure necessary funding. In November 2023, Altman was briefly removed as CEO by the OpenAI board, which stated he had not been "consistently candid in his communications." This triggered a crisis where over 700 employees threatened to resign. He was reinstated as CEO just days later, with the board being restructured (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

Under his leadership, OpenAI has released major products like [[concepts/chatgpt.md]] and the [[concepts/gpt-series.md]], driving the company to a valuation of approximately $850 billion by March 2026 (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
