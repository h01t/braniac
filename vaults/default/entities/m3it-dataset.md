# M3IT Dataset

**Summary**: A large-scale dataset designed for multi-modal multilingual instruction tuning, containing diverse tasks across images and text in multiple languages.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Overview
M3IT (Towards Multi-modal Multi-lingual Instruction Tuning) is a dataset created to advance the field of [[concepts/instruction-tuning.md]] for multimodal models. It aggregates a wide variety of vision-language tasks formatted with instructions.

## Composition
The dataset is large-scale and includes data in multiple languages, aiming to improve the generalization of models across linguistic and cultural boundaries. It provides (instruction, image, output) triplets for training models to follow commands involving visual content (Source: [282]).

## Significance
By providing a centralized, diverse resource, M3IT facilitates research into creating more adaptable and general-purpose [[concepts/vision-language-modeling.md]] that can follow human instructions in various languages.
