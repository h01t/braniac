# Gary Marcus

**Summary**: An AI researcher and prominent critic of large language models, known for highlighting their limitations and criticizing industry practices like benchmark manipulation.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

Gary Marcus is a scientist and author known for his skeptical stance on the current trajectory of AI development, particularly concerning large language models like those from [[entities/openai.md]].

**Criticism of OpenAI**: Marcus has been a vocal critic of OpenAI's benchmarking practices. He called the company's promotion of its [[entities/o3.md]] model "manipulative" after it was revealed that OpenAI funded and had prior access to the "independent" FrontierMath dataset on which o3 set records.

**Technical Skepticism**: Marcus frequently highlights the ongoing limits in reasoning and reliability of AI systems, questioning the scaling hypothesis amid delays and modest benchmark improvements. He represents a critical academic perspective countering the [[concepts/overhype-of-ai.md]] associated with companies like OpenAI.
