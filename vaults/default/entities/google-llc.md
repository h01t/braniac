# Google LLC

**Summary**: Google LLC is an American multinational technology company specializing in internet-based services and products, including online advertising, search engine technology, cloud computing, software, and hardware. It was founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin and became a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc. in 2015.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

Google LLC is a leading technology firm known for its dominant [[concepts/search-engine.md]] and extensive digital ecosystem. The company's history is marked by rapid growth from a Stanford University research project into a global conglomerate.

## History

### Founding and Early Innovations (1996–2003)
[[entities/larry-page.md]] and [[entities/sergey-brin.md]], graduate students at Stanford University, began developing a web search prototype called "BackRub" in early 1996 to study the link structure of the web (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google). They introduced the [[concepts/pagerank.md]] algorithm, which ranked webpages by the quantity and quality of incoming hyperlinks, treating links as endorsements (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google). The domain google.com was registered on September 15, 1997, and Google Inc. incorporated on September 4, 1998, after an initial $100,000 investment from Andy Bechtolsheim (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google). Early operations were run from a garage in Menlo Park.

### Expansion and IPO (2004–2011)
Google went public on August 19, 2004, via a Dutch auction IPO, raising $1.67 billion at an $85 share price (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google). Key acquisitions during this period included [[entities/android-inc.md|Android Inc.]] (2005), YouTube (2006), and DoubleClick (2007). Major product launches included Gmail (2004), Google Maps (2005), and the Chrome browser (2008). Revenue grew from $3.19 billion in 2004 to $29.3 billion in 2010, primarily from advertising (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).

### Maturation and Alphabet Restructuring (2012–Present)
In 2015, Google announced a corporate restructuring, creating [[entities/alphabet-inc.md]] as its parent company to better manage its diverse ventures beyond core internet services (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google). The restructuring took effect on October 2, 2015, with Sundar Pichai becoming CEO of Google and Larry Page becoming CEO of Alphabet. Post-restructuring, the company has heavily invested in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence.md]], cloud computing, and hardware. Revenue surpassed $350 billion by 2024 (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).

## Core Products and Services
Google's portfolio includes its flagship search engine, the [[concepts/advertising-ecosystem.md]] (Google Ads, AdSense), YouTube, Google Cloud, the Android operating system, Pixel smartphones, Nest devices, and numerous AI-driven services. The company's search engine handles billions of queries daily and holds approximately 92% of the global search market share as of 2025 (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).

## Corporate Structure and Leadership
Since 2015, Google has operated as the largest subsidiary of Alphabet Inc. [[entities/sundar-pichai.md]] has served as CEO of Google since 2015 and assumed the role of Alphabet CEO in 2019.

## Financial Performance
Google's revenue is predominantly driven by its advertising business, which accounted for 75-80% of Alphabet's $350 billion revenue in 2024 (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).

## Regulatory and Legal Challenges
Google faces significant regulatory scrutiny, including antitrust actions from the U.S. Department of Justice and other global entities over its search and advertising dominance (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).
