# Qwen

**Summary**: A family of open-source large language models developed by Alibaba, used as base architectures for distilling reasoning capabilities from DeepSeek-R1.
**Source Context**: DeepSeek_R1.pdf

---

## Role in DeepSeek-R1 Distillation

The Qwen models, specifically the Qwen2.5 series, serve as student models in the [[concepts/distillation-reasoning.md]] process. The authors use Qwen2.5 models of various sizes (e.g., 7B, 32B) as base models and fine-tune them on reasoning data generated by [[entities/deepseek-r1.md]].

## Performance of Distilled Models

The distilled Qwen models achieve remarkable reasoning performance:
- **DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B**: Scores 55.5% on AIME 2024, surpassing the larger QwQ-32B-Preview model.
- **DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B**: Scores 72.6% on AIME 2024 and 94.3% on MATH-500, performing comparably to OpenAI's o1-mini.

These results demonstrate the effectiveness of distillation and the strong base capabilities of the Qwen architecture.

## Open-Source Release

The distilled Qwen checkpoints (1.5B, 7B, 14B, 32B, 70B) are open-sourced by DeepSeek-AI, providing the community with powerful, smaller reasoning models.
