# Anthropic

**Summary**: Anthropic is an AI safety and research company that develops general AI systems and language models, with Claude as its flagship assistant.
**Source Context**: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7 (Company context from the announcement source).

---

## Company Mission and Focus
Anthropic is an AI safety and research company whose stated mission is to build reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. The company is known for its focus on developing AI that is helpful, honest, and harmless.

## Key Product: Claude
The company's primary public-facing product is Claude, an AI assistant. Claude is powered by a series of proprietary large language models (LLMs) developed by Anthropic. The latest flagship model in this series is [[entities/claude-opus.md]] 4.7, announced in April 2025. The development and release of this model is a core activity for the entity.

## Context from the Announcement
In the source material, Anthropic is identified as the developer and publisher of the Claude Opus 4.7 model. The news article on their official website serves as the primary channel for announcing this major product update, establishing their role as the creator and steward of the [[concepts/model-performance-upgrades.md]].
