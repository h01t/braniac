# D. Ganguli

**Summary**: A researcher affiliated with Anthropic, known for extensive work on large language model safety, alignment, and red teaming.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

D. Ganguli is a lead author on the influential red teaming study "Red teaming language models to reduce harms: Methods, scaling behaviors, and lessons learned" (2022) [[sources/ganguli-et-al-red-teaming-2022.md]]. This work provides a foundational methodology for [[concepts/safety-red-teaming.md]] and informs [[concepts/alignment.md]] practices.
