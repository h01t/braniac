# Greg Brockman

**Summary**: American technology executive, co-founder and President of OpenAI, and former CTO of Stripe.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

**Greg Brockman** is a co-founder and the President of [[entities/openai.md]]. At the organization's founding in 2015, he served as the Chief Technology Officer (CTO). Prior to OpenAI, Brockman was the CTO of the financial technology company Stripe (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

Brockman played a key role in OpenAI's early technical development and its structural evolution. He resigned from his position as Chairman of the board in November 2023 in protest of CEO [[entities/sam-altman.md]]'s removal. Following Altman's reinstatement, Brockman returned as President (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
