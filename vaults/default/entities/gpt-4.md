# GPT-4

**Summary**: A highly capable large language model developed by OpenAI, known for top-tier performance across a wide range of language understanding and reasoning benchmarks.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf, Table 12 and challenges discussion.

---

## Model Overview
GPT-4 is referenced as a leading model in the LLM landscape. Its specific architecture size is not disclosed in the source ("-" in size column) but it is frequently cited as a top performer.

## Benchmark Performance
According to Table 12, GPT-4 achieved top scores on several major benchmarks:
*   **MMLU**: 86.4% (5-shot)
*   **HellaSwag**: 95.3% (10-shot)
*   **WinoGrande**: 87.5% (5-shot)
*   **GSM8K**: 92.0% (5-shot)
*   **HumanEval**: 67.0% (zero-shot)

## Context in Discussion
GPT-4 is used as a key example when discussing the significant advancements and the subsequent challenges brought by state-of-the-art LLMs [Source, Section 7].
