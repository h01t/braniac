# Google Search

**Summary**: Google Search is the company's flagship web search engine, launched in 1998, which indexes vast online content and holds a dominant global market share of over 90%, forming the foundation of Google's economic and cultural influence.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

Google Search is the core service upon which the company was built and remains its most recognizable and economically critical product.

## Function and Economic Role
Launched in 1998, Google Search organizes the world's information by indexing web content, enabling efficient information discovery and reducing search costs to near-zero. This functionality supported the expansion of the online economy and is credited with generating significant [[concepts/productivity-impact.md|productivity gains]]. According to the source, Google services, led by Search, generated $850 billion in global economic activity in 2024.

## Market Dominance and Antitrust
As of December 2025, Google Search holds a 90.8% global market share. This [[concepts/market-dominance.md|dominant position]] has been the central focus of major [[concepts/antitrust-actions.md|antitrust lawsuits]]. In 2024, a U.S. court ruled this constituted an illegal monopoly, maintained partly through exclusive default agreements with browser and device makers like Apple.

## Competitive Landscape and Evolution
Competitors like Microsoft Bing and DuckDuckGo hold minor shares. The rise of generative AI and conversational agents like [[entities/chatgpt.md|ChatGPT]] presents a new competitive challenge, prompting Google to integrate [[entities/gemini-ai.md|Gemini AI]] features directly into search results. Despite this, network effects, user habit, and the quality of its core algorithm have helped maintain its leadership position.
