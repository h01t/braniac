# OpenAI

**Summary**: An American artificial intelligence research organization and company, founded in 2015, known for developing the GPT series of large language models, ChatGPT, DALL-E, and other frontier AI systems.

**Source Context**: Consolidated from multiple concept pages referencing OpenAI.

---

## Overview
OpenAI was founded as a non-profit with the mission of ensuring that [[concepts/agi.md]] benefits all of humanity. It later adopted a [[concepts/capped-profit-structure.md]] to attract investment while retaining non-profit governance.

## Key Products and Models
- [[concepts/gpt-models.md]] (including GPT-3, GPT-4, GPT-4o)
- [[concepts/chatgpt.md]]
- [[concepts/dall-e.md]] (text-to-image generation)
- [[concepts/codex.md]] (powering GitHub Copilot)
- [[concepts/o-series.md]] (reasoning models)

## Ecosystem and Impact
OpenAI provides a [[concepts/developer-platform.md]] with APIs that serve over one million paying customers. Its models are used across industries for content generation, software development, customer service, and research. The company also faces significant scrutiny regarding [[concepts/ai-safety.md]], [[concepts/ai-ethics.md]], and [[concepts/ai-intellectual-property-disputes.md]].
