# OpenAI

**Summary**: An artificial intelligence research and deployment company, originally founded as a non-profit in 2015 before shifting to a capped-profit structure in 2019. It is known for developing models like GPT, DALL-E, and Sora, and operates the ChatGPT platform.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

OpenAI is a leading company in the field of artificial intelligence. It was established with the mission to ensure that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all of humanity.

## Historical Development
The company's history is marked by significant structural and strategic shifts. It was founded in 2015 as a non-profit research lab. For its early operations and research from 2016 to 2018, see [[concepts/openai-non-profit-era.md]]. A pivotal change occurred in 2019 with its transition to a "capped-profit" model to attract capital for expensive computing resources. This was followed by a period of rapid scaling and key partnerships from 2020 to 2023, and significant ecosystem expansion in 2024 [[concepts/openai-capped-profit-structure.md]].

## Organizational Structure and Leadership
The company has a complex governance structure involving a non-profit board that retains ultimate control over the for-profit subsidiary. Key executives have included Sam Altman. The company experienced significant [[concepts/openai-leadership-instability.md]] in late 2023 with Altman's brief dismissal and subsequent return.

## Core Technologies and Products
OpenAI's primary offerings are built around its series of [[concepts/gpt-models.md]] (Generative Pre-trained Transformers). It also develops multimodal tools like [[concepts/dall-e.md]] for image generation and had developed [[concepts/sora.md]] for video generation, which was reportedly discontinued in 2026. The company provides these models via a robust API and SDK ecosystem for developers.

## Controversies and Public Scrutiny
The company has been involved in numerous public debates and incidents. These include ongoing [[concepts/ai-intellectual-property-disputes.md]] related to data acquisition for training, criticisms over transparency and benchmarking, and ethical lapses in content moderation. Specific recent incidents (2024-2026) involved user harm, policy backlash, and a controversial 2026 agreement with the [[entities/us-department-of-defense.md]], which sparked significant public debate [[concepts/openai-dod-agreement-2026.md]]. The company has also been active in policy circles, with reports of an "AI lobbying frenzy" in Washington.

## Business and Societal Impact
OpenAI's innovations, particularly ChatGPT, have driven significant productivity gains and scientific progress. However, these advancements are accompanied by criticisms of overhyping AI capabilities, concerns about job displacement, and the company's substantial influence on global AI policy.
