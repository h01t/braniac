# Zhipu AI

**Summary**: Zhipu AI is a Chinese AI startup, spun out of Tsinghua University, renowned for its long-standing open-source ChatGLM/GLM series of bilingual [[concepts/large-language-model.md]]s, culminating in the large-scale GLM-4.5 MoE models released in 2025.
**Source Context**: https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/chinese-open-source-llms-2025

---

## Model Lineage
Zhipu AI's **ChatGLM** series (e.g., ChatGLM-6B, ChatGLM2-6B, ChatGLM3-6B) are bilingual (English/Chinese) chatbots released under permissive Apache-2.0 licenses and have been downloaded millions of times worldwide.

## 2025 MoE Releases
In July 2025, Zhipu released significantly larger models based on a [[concepts/mixture-of-experts.md]] architecture:
*   **GLM-4.5**: A 355B parameter model.
*   **GLM-4.5-Air**: A 106B parameter model.
Reuters reported these as "the most advanced open-source MoE model[s]" in China at the time [^5][^6].

## Strategic Role
Zhipu's roadmap is closely tied to Chinese AI policy. The company has been dubbed one of China’s "AI tigers," and its open-LLM efforts are seen as helping the country fulfill governmental goals for AI leadership [^5]. Its releases contribute significantly to the scale of the [[concepts/chinese-open-source-llm-landscape-2025.md]].
