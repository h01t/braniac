# J. Li

**Summary**: A researcher known for work on vision-language models, particularly as a lead author of the BLIP-2 paper which proposed an efficient method for bootstrapping multimodal pre-training.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

J. Li is the first author of "BLIP-2: Bootstrapping language-image pre-training with frozen image encoders and large language models" (2023) [[sources/li-et-al-blip-2-2023.md]]. This work is a major contribution to [[concepts/multimodal-models.md]], providing a scalable and efficient recipe for creating powerful vision-language models by bridging frozen components.
