# Baichuan AI

**Summary**: Baichuan AI is a Chinese startup that has released a series of open-source [[concepts/large-language-model.md]]s, including the Baichuan-7B and Baichuan-13B models, under permissive licenses since 2023.
**Source Context**: https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/chinese-open-source-llms-2025

---

## Key Releases
The company's notable open-source releases include:
*   **Baichuan-7B** and **Baichuan-13B**: Both support Chinese and English and were released under Apache-2.0 licenses in mid-2023, explicitly described as "open-source" by the company [^25].
*   **Baichuan-2**: A larger 33B model announced in late 2023/early 2024 and also released as open-source [^26].

## Market Position
Baichuan's 13B model was noteworthy in China as a sizable open model that rivaled Western efforts at the time. The company is cited among the startups strong in open LLMs within the broader [[concepts/chinese-open-source-llm-landscape-2025.md]] [^2]. Its models are publicly hosted on GitHub and Hugging Face.
