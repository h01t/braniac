# U.S. Department of Defense

**Summary**: The federal department responsible for coordinating and supervising all agencies and functions of the U.S. government directly related to national security and the armed forces.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI (mentioned in context of 2026 agreement)

---

The Department of Defense (DoD) is a major entity in U.S. national security policy. In the context of AI development, it has become a significant driver of research funding and a potential customer for advanced AI systems.

## Involvement with AI and OpenAI
The DoD has publicly stated its intent to integrate artificial intelligence across its operations for purposes such as logistics, intelligence analysis, cyber defense, and training. Its reported 2026 agreement with [[entities/openai.md]] signifies a growing intersection between cutting-edge commercial AI labs and national defense infrastructure. This partnership, detailed in [[concepts/openai-dod-agreement-2026.md]], was controversial as it represented a shift in OpenAI's policy against military applications and raised broader questions about the militarization of advanced AI and its governance.
