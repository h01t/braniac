# MATH-500

**Summary**: A dataset of 500 challenging math problems, used as a benchmark to evaluate the mathematical reasoning of language models.
**Source Context**: DeepSeek_R1.pdf (Table 5, Table 6).

---

## Benchmark Role
[[entities/math-500.md]] is listed alongside [[entities/aime-2024.md]] as a core mathematical reasoning benchmark in the evaluation tables. It is likely a curated or subset version of larger math datasets.

## Performance Context
Models achieved high scores on this benchmark, with the best distilled models scoring above 90% (e.g., 94.3% for [[entities/deepseek-r1-distill-qwen-32b.md]]). It shows the strong mathematical capability transferred via [[concepts/knowledge-distillation.md]].
