# DeepMind

**Summary**: DeepMind is a leading British artificial intelligence research laboratory acquired by Google in 2014, known for breakthroughs in reinforcement learning, game-playing AI, and scientific discovery.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

[[entities/deepmind.md|DeepMind]] was founded in 2010 and acquired by [[entities/google-llc.md|Google]] in January 2014 for a reported sum exceeding $500 million (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google). It operates under the parent company [[entities/alphabet-inc.md]] and is renowned for its work in advanced [[concepts/artificial-intelligence.md]].

## Major Achievements
*   **AlphaGo (2016)**: A program that used deep reinforcement learning and Monte Carlo tree search to defeat world champion Lee Sedol at the complex board game Go, a landmark moment in AI.
*   **AlphaFold (2020)**: A system that accurately predicts the 3D structures of proteins, a critical problem in biology. Its predictions for nearly 200 million proteins were released openly, accelerating scientific research (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).
*   **Transformer Architecture (2017)**: DeepMind researchers were co-authors of the seminal "Attention Is All You Need" paper that introduced the Transformer model, the basis for most modern large language models.
*   **Scientific AI**: DeepMind applies AI to challenges in fields like quantum chemistry, nuclear fusion control, and weather prediction (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).

## Organizational Structure
In 2023, Google merged its Brain AI team with DeepMind to form a single unit called "Google DeepMind," led by DeepMind co-founder Demis Hassabis, to consolidate its AI research efforts (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).
