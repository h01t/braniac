# ACM

**Summary**: The Association for Computing Machinery, a leading professional organization that publishes conferences and journals on computing, including AI and LLM research.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

ACM conferences and journals feature significant research on large language models. E. M. Bender et al.'s work on stochastic parrots appears in the Proceedings of the 2021 ACM conference on fairness, accountability, and transparency [Source: On the dangers of stochastic parrots]. Surveys on LLMs in finance by Y. Li et al. are presented at the ACM International Conference on AI in Finance [Source: Large language models in finance: A survey].

ACM also addresses ethical aspects, such as privacy in language models by H. Brown et al. [Source: What does it mean for a language model to preserve privacy?]. The organization promotes standards and discussions on responsible AI development [[concepts/ethical-considerations-llms.md]].
