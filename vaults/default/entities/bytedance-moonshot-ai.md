# ByteDance (Moonshot AI) - Kimi Series

**Summary**: Moonshot AI, an AI spin-off of ByteDance, produces the open-source Kimi series of [[concepts/large-language-model.md]]s, known for large context windows (128K tokens) and multimodal capabilities, with the K2 model released in July 2025.
**Source Context**: https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/chinese-open-source-llms-2025

---

## Model Development
Moonshot AI's *Kimi* series began with the K1 model and an upgraded **Kimi K1.5** in 2024, offering 20B+ parameter multimodal capabilities [^10]. In July 2025, the company unveiled **Kimi K2**, which featured further improvements in code and reasoning [^10]. Reuters notes that Kimi K2 excels in coding tasks [^10].

## Open-Source Strategy and Features
The Kimi line is released under an open-source strategy, with Moonshot explicitly "follows the example set by Meta" in open-sourcing its advanced models to enhance technological credibility [^11]. A key technical distinction of the Kimi models is their very large context window of 128K tokens and support for multimodal input (e.g., image+text) [^10][^12].

## Market Context
The release of Kimi K2 was part of Moonshot AI's effort to reclaim market position amid intense competition within the [[concepts/chinese-open-source-llm-landscape-2025.md]] [^10].
