# Baidu (Ernie Series)

**Summary**: Baidu is a Chinese tech giant whose historically proprietary Ernie series of [[concepts/large-language-model.md]]s transitioned to an open-source model in 2025 due to intense market competition, with its latest Ernie model made available from end-June 2025.
**Source Context**: https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/chinese-open-source-llms-2025

---

## Transition to Open-Source
Baidu's Ernie models were historically proprietary. However, due to intense competition in the [[concepts/chinese-open-source-llm-landscape-2025.md]], Baidu announced in early 2025 that its latest **Ernie** model would be made open-source and available from the end of June 2025 [^13]. Concurrently, its Ernie Bot chatbot was made free to users starting April 2025 [^14].

## Significance
While Ernie had seen limited adoption relative to newer Chinese challengers, its open-sourcing is significant as it brings one of China’s original and prominent AI models into the open ecosystem [^13]. Baidu also announced plans for Ernie 5, a model with multimodal capabilities, in the second half of 2025 [^15].
