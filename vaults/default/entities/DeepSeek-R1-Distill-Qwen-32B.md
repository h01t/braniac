# DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B
A 32-billion parameter dense model with its reasoning capabilities distilled from the larger [[entities/DeepSeek-R1.md]] teacher model.

**Key Findings:**
*   Achieved superior performance (e.g., 72.6% on AIME) compared to the pure RL-trained counterpart **[[entities/DeepSeek-R1-Zero-Qwen-32B.md]]** (47.0% on AIME).
*   Significantly exceeded the performance of [[entities/OpenAI-o1-mini.md]] on most benchmarks.
*   This comparison between distillation and pure RL highlights the effectiveness and efficiency of the **[[concepts/model-distillation.md]]** approach.
