# Google Walkout 2018

**Summary**: A global walkout on November 1, 2018, where approximately 20,000 Google employees protested the company's handling of sexual harassment allegations and misconduct, particularly regarding executives.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

The Google Walkout for Real Change was a coordinated employee protest across Google offices worldwide. It was organized in response to a New York Times report detailing how Google had protected executives accused of [[concepts/sexual-harassment.md]], including providing large departure packages.

**Employee Demands**
The walkout organizers presented five core demands:
1. An end to forced arbitration for all employee disputes, starting with sexual harassment and assault claims.
2. A commitment to end pay and opportunity inequity.
3. A publicly disclosed sexual harassment transparency report.
4. A clear, uniform, globally inclusive process for reporting sexual misconduct safely and anonymously.
5. The elevation of the Chief Diversity Officer to answer directly to the CEO and make recommendations directly to the board.

**Google's Response**
In response to the walkout, Google made several policy changes, including ending forced arbitration for individual sexual harassment and assault claims. The company also committed to improving its internal reporting processes and hiring an external consultant to review its handling of such cases. However, it did not meet all demands, such as creating a public victims' fund or removing all implicated executives from leadership.

The walkout is a landmark event in tech worker [[concepts/labor-organizing.md]] and corporate accountability for workplace culture.
