# MiniMax M2.7

**Summary**: MiniMax M2.7 is a text model released by MiniMax that demonstrates early signs of self-evolution and excels in software engineering, professional work, and entertainment tasks.

**Source Context**: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en

---

## Overview

MiniMax M2.7 is a model capable of building complex agent harnesses and participating in its own evolution process, as described in the official announcement. It was released on March 18, 2026 (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).

## Key Capabilities

### Self-Evolution

M2.7 can update its own memory and build skills to assist in reinforcement learning experiments, initiating a cycle of [[concepts/self-evolution.md]] (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).

### Software Engineering

On the [[concepts/benchmarks.md#swe-pro|SWE-Pro benchmark]], M2.7 scored 56.22%, approaching the level of Opus models. It also performs well on [[concepts/benchmarks.md#vibe-pro|VIBE-Pro]] (55.6%) and [[concepts/benchmarks.md#terminal-bench-2|Terminal Bench 2]] (57.0%) (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).

### Professional Work

In the GDPval-AA evaluation, M2.7 achieved an ELO score of 1495, the highest among open-source models. It shows improved abilities in Office suite editing and can handle multi-round revisions (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).

### Entertainment

M2.7 has enhanced character consistency and emotional intelligence, enabling applications in interactive entertainment, such as the [[entities/openroom-project.md|OpenRoom]] demo (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).

## Availability

M2.7 is available on [[entities/minimax-agent.md|MiniMax Agent]] and the [[entities/minimax-api-platform.md|MiniMax API Platform]] (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).
