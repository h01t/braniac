# ChatGPT Health

**Summary**: A dedicated, private space within ChatGPT for health-related conversations that allows secure integration of medical records and wellness app data, launched in early 2026.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Features and Launch

ChatGPT Health is a private space within [[entities/products/chatgpt.md]] designed for health conversations. It allows users in the US to securely connect medical records via b.well and integrate data from wellness apps like [[entities/technology/apple-health.md]], Function Health, [[entities/companies/peloton.md]], and MyFitnessPal [208].

Users can provide custom health instructions. All health data is encrypted and excluded from model training. The feature incorporated feedback from over 260 physicians [208].

It initially rolled out to a waitlist of users on web and iOS for Free, Go, Plus, and Pro plans, excluding the EEA, Switzerland, and UK. Android support was planned for the future [208].

## Use Cases and Compliance

The tool provides personalized support for explaining medical test results, preparing for doctor visits, diet/workout planning, and comparing insurance options. Health data is isolated from regular ChatGPT conversations [208].

Concurrently, OpenAI introduced **OpenAI for Healthcare**, a suite including a [[concepts/hipaa-compliance.md]]-compliant ChatGPT for Healthcare product targeted at clinical settings. It is being rolled out to major institutions including AdventHealth, Boston Children’s Hospital, and Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center [209].
