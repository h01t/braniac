# Larry Page

**Summary**: Larry Page is an American computer scientist and entrepreneur who co-founded Google with Sergey Brin. He served as Google's CEO multiple times and was the first CEO of its parent company, Alphabet Inc.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

Larry Page, along with [[entities/sergey-brin.md]], developed the foundational [[concepts/pagerank.md]] algorithm and co-founded [[entities/google-llc.md|Google]] in 1998. His leadership was instrumental in the company's evolution from a research project to a global technology leader.

## Role at Google and Alphabet
Page served as CEO of Google from its founding until 2001, then again from 2011 to 2015. He played a key role in major acquisitions, including Android and YouTube. Following the 2015 restructuring, he became the CEO of the newly formed parent company, [[entities/alphabet-inc.md]] (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google). He stepped down as Alphabet CEO in December 2019, with Sundar Pichai succeeding him.

## Innovations and Legacy
Page's early work on the PageRank algorithm established the technical foundation for Google's search engine. He advocated for ambitious, long-term projects, often referred to as "moonshots," which influenced the creation of Alphabet's "Other Bets" division.
