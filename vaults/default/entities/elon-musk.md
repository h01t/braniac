# Elon Musk

**Summary**: Entrepreneur and CEO of SpaceX and Tesla, and a co-founder and early donor and advisor to OpenAI.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

**Elon Musk** is a co-founder of [[entities/openai.md]]. At its inception in December 2015, he served as a co-chair alongside [[entities/sam-altman.md]]. Musk was a primary early donor, contributing approximately $50 million to the non-profit, and helped recruit early talent and foster initial ties with [[entities/microsoft.md]] (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

Musk's motivation for co-founding OpenAI was rooted in concerns about the existential risks of [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence.md]] (AGI) and a desire to create a counterweight to large, profit-driven companies like [[entities/google.md]]. He has since been a vocal critic of OpenAI's evolution, arguing that its shift to a for-profit, Microsoft-influenced entity represents a departure from its original open and safety-focused mission (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
