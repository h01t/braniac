# WavCaps Dataset

**Summary**: A large-scale, weakly-labelled audio captioning dataset created with ChatGPT assistance to facilitate audio-language multimodal research.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Description
WavCaps is a dataset designed for training and evaluating models on the task of [[concepts/audio-captioning.md]]. It contains audio clips paired with textual captions. The "weakly-labelled" aspect indicates the captions are not professionally curated but are sourced and processed from the web.

## Creation Methodology
The dataset was constructed using ChatGPT to assist in filtering and refining raw audio-text pairs collected from the internet. This semi-automated process helps ensure higher quality captions at scale (Source: [276]).

## Purpose and Use
WavCaps serves as a resource for training models in the broader field of [[concepts/multimodal-language-modeling.md]], particularly for tasks that require understanding and generating language from auditory input. It addresses the need for large, diverse audio-text data.
