# EU AI Act

**Summary**: A comprehensive regulatory framework for artificial intelligence in the European Union, classifying systems by risk and imposing strict requirements on high-risk AI like OpenAI's models.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

The EU AI Act is a landmark regulation that became effective in August 2024. It deems advanced AI systems, such as those developed by [[entities/openai.md]], as high-risk, subjecting them to rigorous transparency, safety, and fundamental rights assessments.

OpenAI's engagement with the Act has been contentious. In 2025, CEO [[entities/sam-altman.md]] warned that the Act's strictures might limit European access to AI, potentially benefiting less-regulated regions. In October 2025, OpenAI raised antitrust issues with EU authorities, seeking measures against integrated tech giants' infrastructure dominance.

The company also backed the EU's July 2025 voluntary Code of Practice but highlighted burdens on adoption, compute, and data. The Act represents a central pillar of the global [[concepts/regulatory-challenges.md]] facing AI developers.
