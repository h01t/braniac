# CoT-PT

**Summary**: A Chain of Thought prompting method that uses prompt tuning and specific visual bias to generate reasoning implicitly.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

CoT-PT (Chain of Thought Prompt Tuning) is a [[concepts/prompting-for-mllms.md]] technique. It applies both prompt tuning and specific visual bias to generate a chain of reasoning implicitly for solving multimodal tasks [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf].
