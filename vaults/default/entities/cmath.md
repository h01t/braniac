# CMath
A Chinese math benchmark.

**Evaluation Context:**
*   **Method:** [[concepts/generation_evaluation.md]] (3-shot for base models).
*   **Performance:** DeepSeek 67B achieves 63.0.
*   **Insight:** [[entities/llama2.md]] demonstrates strong performance (53.9) despite no specific Chinese training, indicating mathematical reasoning ability can be effectively transferred across languages.

**Source:** Wei et al., 2023.
