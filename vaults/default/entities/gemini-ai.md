# Gemini AI

**Summary**: Gemini (formerly Duet AI) is Google's suite of advanced artificial intelligence models integrated into its Workspace and Cloud platforms, designed to automate routine tasks like summarization, drafting, and data analysis to boost user productivity.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

Gemini represents Google's strategic push to embed generative AI capabilities across its product ecosystem, competing with rivals like OpenAI's ChatGPT.

## Functionality and Integration
Gemini is integrated into [[entities/google-workspace.md|Google Workspace]] (e.g., helping draft emails in Gmail or generate summaries in Docs) and [[entities/google-cloud-platform.md|Google Cloud Platform]] (e.g., via Vertex AI for model development). It automates routine knowledge-worker tasks such as content creation, code generation, and data analysis.

## Measured Impact
According to Google internal metrics cited in the source, Gemini users were 20% more likely to complete their work on time. In enterprise settings, Google Cloud's Vertex AI yielded a 397% return on investment over three years by accelerating AI model development and deployment. These tools contribute to broader estimates that generative AI could add trillions annually to global productivity.

## Competitive Context
The rise of AI challengers like [[entities/chatgpt.md|ChatGPT]] has pressured Google's core search business, leading the company to integrate Gemini features (like AI overviews) directly into search results to maintain its competitive position in [[concepts/market-dominance.md|the search market]].
