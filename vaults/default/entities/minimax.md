# MiniMax

**Summary**: MiniMax is a Shanghai-based AI startup that launched its open-source, low-cost MiniMax-01 LLM family in January 2025, including general text and multimodal variants, aiming to rival top U.S. models.
**Source Context**: https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/chinese-open-source-llms-2025

---

## Model Family
In January 2025, MiniMax launched its *MiniMax-01* LLM family, which includes:
*   **MiniMax-Text-01**: A general open LLM.
*   **MiniMax-VL-01**: A multimodal model handling text and vision [^23].

## Performance and Strategy
The company's benchmarks claimed parity with leading AI models in math, reasoning, and instruction following. Its announcement emphasized that the models are **low-cost and open-source**, aiming to rival U.S. tech offerings. They were released with permissive licenses on Chinese repositories [^23]. MiniMax's debut underscores a trend within the [[concepts/chinese-open-source-llm-landscape-2025.md]] where Chinese startups tout the cost-effectiveness of their open models compared to expensive alternatives [^23].
