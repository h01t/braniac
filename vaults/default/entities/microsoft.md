# Microsoft

**Summary**: A critical long-term strategic partner and investor in OpenAI, providing a substantial portion of its financing and compute infrastructure, while also benefiting from the partnership through significant Azure cloud revenue growth.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Relationship with OpenAI
Microsoft is OpenAI's most significant business partner. As of March 2026, OpenAI disclosed that Microsoft supplies "a substantial portion of our financing and compute," identifying this dependence as a major business risk [Source]. Despite this risk, OpenAI reaffirmed that "Microsoft is and will remain a critical long-term partner" [Source]. Microsoft's diluted stake in OpenAI was approximately 27% after recent funding rounds [Source].

## Business Impact
The partnership has driven substantial growth for Microsoft's cloud business. In Q2 FY2026, Microsoft reported $51.5 billion in cloud revenue, growth attributed in part to support for OpenAI's expansion and projects like the [[concepts/stargate-project.md]] [Source].
