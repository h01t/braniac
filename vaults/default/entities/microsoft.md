# Microsoft

**Summary**: A global technology company and the primary strategic partner and investor in OpenAI, holding a significant equity stake and an extensive commercial agreement involving Azure cloud services and revenue sharing.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Partnership with OpenAI
Microsoft's partnership with [[entities/openai.md]] began with a $1 billion investment in 2019 and expanded to approximately $13 billion by 2023 (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI). This partnership provides OpenAI with exclusive access to Azure for model training and deployment.

## Revised Agreement (October 2025)
As part of an October 2025 restructuring, Microsoft secured approximately a 27% stake in OpenAI. The revised agreement included (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI):
*   Extension of Microsoft's revenue share to 20% of OpenAI's total revenue until 2032 (previously until 2030).
*   An OpenAI commitment to an incremental $250 billion in Azure cloud services purchases.
*   Extension of Microsoft's exclusive API access and IP rights through 2032 (with exclusivity until AGI is achieved).

## Financial Benefit & Strategic Shift
The partnership is highly lucrative for Microsoft, providing potential stake appreciation, ongoing revenue share (over $5 billion annually at early 2026 revenue levels), and locked-in Azure revenue (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

In February 2026, Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman signaled a shift toward reducing reliance on OpenAI by developing the company's own frontier-scale AI models, expected to launch in 2026 (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI). However, a joint statement on March 2, 2026, reaffirmed the ongoing collaboration with no changes to partnership terms.
