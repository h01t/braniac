# Microsoft

**Summary**: American multinational technology corporation and a major investor and strategic partner of OpenAI.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

**Microsoft** is a key strategic partner and the largest minority shareholder in [[entities/openai.md]], holding a 27% stake as of March 2026 (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

The partnership began in 2019, with Microsoft providing critical cloud computing infrastructure and investment to support OpenAI's scaling efforts. Microsoft's Azure cloud is the exclusive provider for OpenAI's AI supercomputing needs. The partnership deepened with a multiyear, multibillion-dollar investment in January 2023, reported to be $10 billion (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

This collaboration has been foundational for OpenAI's ability to train large models like the [[concepts/gpt-series.md]] and to scale products like [[concepts/chatgpt.md]]. Microsoft has also integrated OpenAI's technology into its own products, such as [[concepts/github-copilot.md]], which is powered by OpenAI's Codex model (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
