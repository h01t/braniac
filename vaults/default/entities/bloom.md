# BLOOM

**Summary**: A causal decoder model trained on the ROOTS corpus, designed as an open-source LLM with architectural improvements.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf, Batch 2 of 15.

---

BLOOM is an open-source LLM with key details:

- **Architecture**: Causal decoder with ALiBi positional embedding and an additional normalization layer after the embedding layer, enhancing training stability and downstream performance [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf].

- **Training**: Trained on the ROOTS corpus to provide an accessible large language model for the community [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf].

- **Features**: Architectural changes, such as those suggested by the bitsandbytes library, differentiate it from standard transformers [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf].

BLOOM contributes to discussions on [[concepts/adaptation-stages.md]] and open-source model development.
