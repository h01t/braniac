# MiniMax

**Summary**: MiniMax is a company that develops AI models and products, with a mission of "Intelligence with Everyone."

**Source Context**: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en

---

## Overview

MiniMax is the creator of the M2-series models, including M2.7. The company offers products like [[entities/minimax-agent.md|MiniMax Agent]] and an API platform (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).

## Products and Services

Based on the blog post, MiniMax provides models for text, speech, video, and music, as well as AI-native applications and developer APIs (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).
