# Gemini

**Summary**: A family of multi-modal large language models from Google, with Gemini-1 achieving human-level MMLU performance and Gemini-1.5 featuring a massive 2M token context window.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Model Evolution and Capabilities
Gemini is Google's family of multi-modal LLMs, succeeding Bard (which was based on PaLM).
*   **Gemini-1**: Noted as the first auto-regressive model to achieve human-level capabilities on the MMLU (Massive Multitask Language Understanding) benchmark.
*   **Gemini-1.5**: A more advanced, multi-modal model built as a [[concepts/mixture-of-experts.md]] (MoE) architecture based on Gemini-1's findings. Its most standout feature is an extremely large context window of **2 million tokens**, with the ability to reason over up to 10 million tokens. This was an unprecedented achievement at the time and was shown to have a huge impact on performance gains.
