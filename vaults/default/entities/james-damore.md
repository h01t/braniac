# James Damore

**Summary**: A former Google engineer who was fired in 2017 after writing an internal memo critiquing the company's diversity efforts, which sparked a major public debate on workplace speech and ideology.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

James Damore was a software engineer at Google. In 2017, he authored and circulated an internal memo titled "Google's Ideological Echo Chamber," which critiqued the company's diversity initiatives. The memo argued that biological differences between men and women might explain some of the gender gap in tech roles and criticized what he perceived as an [[concepts/ideological-monoculture.md]] at Google.

Google CEO [[entities/sundar-pichai.md]] fired Damore, stating the memo violated company policy by "perpetuating harmful gender stereotypes" and creating a hostile work environment. Damore subsequently sued Google for discrimination and retaliation. The lawsuit was settled in 2020 without Google admitting fault.

The case became a flashpoint for debates about [[concepts/workplace-free-speech.md]], the limits of corporate policy, and the intersection of science and ideology in [[concepts/diversity-and-inclusion.md]] programs. Damore argued his memo was based on scientific literature and raised concerns about political bias.
