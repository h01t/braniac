# OpenAI Group

**Summary**: The public benefit corporation (PBC) formed in October 2025 as the restructured for-profit entity of OpenAI, controlled by the nonprofit OpenAI Foundation.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Formation and Structure
In October 2025, OpenAI restructured its for-profit entity into the OpenAI Group public benefit corporation (PBC) under the nonprofit [[entities/openai-foundation.md]] [[sources/ref-20.md]].
The [[entities/openai-foundation.md]] retains significant equity and full governance rights via board appointment control over the PBC [[sources/ref-20.md]].

## Financial Stake
The restructuring included [[entities/microsoft.md]] receiving a 27% stake in the OpenAI Group PBC, valued at approximately $135 billion, with extended intellectual property access until 2032.
