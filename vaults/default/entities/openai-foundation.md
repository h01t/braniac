# OpenAI Foundation

**Summary**: The nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization founded in 2015 that maintains ultimate control over OpenAI's for-profit arm and its mission to develop beneficial AGI.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Governance and Control
OpenAI's governance is directed by the board of directors of its nonprofit entity, the OpenAI Foundation [[sources/ref-20.md]]. It is a 501(c)(3) organization founded in 2015 that maintains ultimate control over the for-profit arm, now the [[entities/openai-group.md]] PBC [[sources/ref-20.md]].
The Foundation holds fiduciary responsibility to advance the mission of developing [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence.md]] (AGI) that benefits humanity [[sources/ref-20.md]]. It owns about 26% equity in the PBC [[sources/ref-20.md]].

## Board Composition
As of May 2025, the board includes independent directors such as Chair [[entities/bret-taylor.md]], [[entities/adam-d-angelo.md]], and others with expertise in technology, policy, and safety, excluding OpenAI executives for impartiality [[sources/ref-20.md]].
