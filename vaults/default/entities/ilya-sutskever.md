# Ilya Sutskever

**Summary**: Computer scientist and co-founder of OpenAI, who served as its Chief Scientist and was a research director at the time of founding.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

**Ilya Sutskever** is a co-founder of [[entities/openai.md]] and served as its Chief Scientist. At the organization's founding in 2015, he was the research director, having been recruited from [[entities/google.md]] where he worked on deep learning (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

Sutskever is a leading figure in machine learning research and has been central to the development of OpenAI's core technologies, including the [[concepts/gpt-series.md]]. He was a member of the nonprofit board that briefly ousted CEO [[entities/sam-altman.md]] in November 2023 (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
