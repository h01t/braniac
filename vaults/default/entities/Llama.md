# Llama (Llama3)
**Source:** [[sources/DeepSeek_R1.md]]

Llama (specifically Llama3) is a series of open-source language models from Meta. In the DeepSeek-R1 paper, Llama models are used as base models for **[[concepts/Distillation_Reasoning.md|distillation]]**.

## Role in DeepSeek-R1
- **Distillation Base:** Alongside **[[entities/Qwen.md]]**, Llama models are fine-tuned with reasoning data from **[[entities/DeepSeek_R1.md]]**.
- **Open Source:** The paper releases distilled checkpoints based on Llama series at various sizes (1.5B, 7B, 8B, 14B, 32B, 70B).

## Note
The extracted text does not provide detailed evaluation results for Llama-based distilled models, but they are part of the open-source release.
