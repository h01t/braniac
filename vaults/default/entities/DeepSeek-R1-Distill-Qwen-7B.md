# DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B
A 7-billion parameter dense model with its reasoning capabilities distilled from the larger [[entities/DeepSeek-R1.md]] teacher model.

**Key Findings:**
*   An example of successful **[[concepts/model-distillation.md]]**.
*   **Outperformed non-reasoning models like GPT-4o-0513** across all evaluated [[concepts/reasoning-benchmarks.md]].
*   Referred to as **DeepSeek-R1-7B** in abbreviated form in the source.

**Significance:** Demonstrates that distillation can efficiently transfer strong reasoning performance into much smaller models.
