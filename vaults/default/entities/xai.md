# xAI

**Summary**: An artificial intelligence company founded by Elon Musk, responsible for developing the Grok family of large language models.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf.

---

## Overview
xAI is a company focused on developing artificial intelligence, with a stated goal of building AI to "advance our collective understanding of the universe." It was founded by Elon Musk and announced in July 2023 (Source: General Knowledge; specific mention in source PDF for model origin).

## Key Product: Grok LLMs
The primary public output from xAI discussed in the source is the **Grok family** of large language models. This includes:
*   **Grok-1**: A 314-billion parameter [[concepts/mixture-of-experts.md]] model.
*   **Grok-1.5**: An enhanced, multi-modal version with a larger context length and improved performance (Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf).
