# arXiv

**Summary**: A preprint repository for scholarly papers in fields like physics, mathematics, and computer science, widely used for disseminating LLM research.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

arXiv hosts numerous preprints on large language models, facilitating rapid sharing of research findings. For example, J. B. Tenenbaum et al.'s paper on mathematics evaluation is available as arXiv:2306.01694 [Source: Evaluating language models for mathematics through interactions]. Similarly, surveys on hallucination by Y. Zhang et al. (arXiv:2309.01219) and security challenges by B. C. Das et al. (arXiv:2402.00888) are published on arXiv.

The platform supports the AI community by providing open access to cutting-edge studies, including those on legal benchmarks [[concepts/legal-applications-llms.md]] and robotics applications [[concepts/robotics-llms.md]].
