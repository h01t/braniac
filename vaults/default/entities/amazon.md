# Amazon

**Summary**: A multinational technology company and, as of February 2026, a strategic investor and exclusive third-party cloud distribution partner for OpenAI via Amazon Web Services (AWS).
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Partnership with OpenAI
On February 27, 2026, [[entities/openai.md]] announced a strategic partnership with Amazon and AWS, designating AWS as the exclusive third-party cloud distribution partner for OpenAI models (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

## Investment
As part of the same announcement, Amazon committed a $50 billion investment into OpenAI's $110 billion funding round. This commitment was structured as $15 billion initial, with $35 billion additional contingent on conditions, potentially tied to an AGI milestone or IPO (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
