# Robert Epstein

**Summary**: An American psychologist and researcher known for his studies on the "search engine manipulation effect" (SEME), testifying that Google's algorithms can significantly influence voter preferences.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

Robert Epstein is a senior research psychologist at the American Institute for Behavioral Research and Technology. He is best known in technology circles for his work on the potential influence of [[concepts/search-engine-algorithm.md|search algorithms]] on human behavior, particularly in politics.

**Research on Search Influence**
Epstein's key research involves the "search engine manipulation effect" (SEME). His experiments suggest that the way search engines like [[entities/google.md]] rank results can shift the preferences of undecided voters by 20% or more. He argues this influence is powerful because it is subtle and users are largely unaware of it.

**Congressional Testimony**
In 2019, Epstein testified before the U.S. Senate Judiciary Committee, stating that his monitoring indicated Google's search systems curbed pro-Republican autocomplete suggestions and search results during the 2018 midterm elections. He posited this could have affected outcomes in tight races.

His work is central to debates about [[concepts/search-engine-bias.md]], [[concepts/algorithmic-transparency.md]], and the potential for technology companies to wield significant, opaque influence over public opinion and democratic processes.
