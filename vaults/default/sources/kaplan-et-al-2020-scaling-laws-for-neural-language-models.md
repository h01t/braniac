# Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models
**Authors:** Kaplan, J., McCandlish, S., Henighan, T., Brown, T. B., Chess, B., Child, R., Gray, S., Radford, A., Wu, J., and Amodei, D.
**Venue:** CoRR, abs/2001.08361
**Year:** 2020
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361

## Summary
Empirical study of scaling laws for neural language models, examining how model performance scales with compute, data, and model size.
