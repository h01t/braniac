## Content Overview
This technical report introduces and comprehensively describes [[entities/kimi-k2-5.md]]. Key sections include:

2.  **Joint Optimization of Text and Vision**: Details the core training paradigm involving [[concepts/native-multimodal-pretraining.md]], [[concepts/zero-vision-sft.md]], and [[concepts/joint-multimodal-rl.md]].

## Key Claims and Findings
*   Kimi K2.5 achieves state-of-the-art results on various benchmarks.
*   Joint optimization leads to bidirectional enhancement of text and vision capabilities.
*   Agent Swarm reduces latency by up to 4.5x and improves accuracy over single-agent baselines.
*   The model checkpoint is released open-source at https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K2.5.
