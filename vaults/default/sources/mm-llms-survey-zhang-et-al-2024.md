# MM-LLMs: Recent Advances in Multimodal Large Language Models (Zhang et al., 2024)

**Summary**: A survey paper reviewing the rapid progress in Multimodal Large Language Models, covering model architectures, training techniques, capabilities, applications, and future directions.
**Source Context**: Fundamentals of Building Autonomous LLM Agents.pdf (Ref 67: Zhang et al., 2024. MM-LLMs: Recent advances in multimodal large language models. arXiv:2401.13601).

---

## Coverage of the Field
This survey provides a structured overview of the [[concepts/multimodal-llms.md]] landscape. It likely categorizes models by their architectural approach (e.g., encoder-decoder, fusion methods), training data, and modalities handled (vision, audio, video). It discusses key techniques like visual tokenization, alignment strategies (inspired by **CLIP**), and instruction tuning. The survey also probably covers prominent models (GPT-4V, LLaVA, etc.), evaluation benchmarks, and applications.

## Value for Agent Developers
For builders of [[concepts/autonomous-llm-agents.md]], understanding MM-LLMs is essential because visual perception is a gateway to interacting with most real-world digital and physical environments. This survey offers a roadmap to selecting, understanding, and potentially extending the vision capabilities of an agent. It connects core multimodal research to downstream applications like robotics, healthcare, and **screen agents**.

## Relation to Other Surveys
It complements other surveys in the bibliography, such as the one on tool-augmented generation (Gao et al., 2024) and the comprehensive agent survey (Wang et al., 2025), by providing depth on the perceptual module that many advanced agents rely on.
