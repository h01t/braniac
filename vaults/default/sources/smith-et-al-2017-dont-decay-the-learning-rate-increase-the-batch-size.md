**Authors:** Smith, S. L., Kindermans, P.-J., Ying, C., and Le, Q. V.
**Venue:** arXiv preprint arXiv:1711.00489
**Year:** 2017

## Summary
Proposes increasing batch size instead of decaying learning rate for training neural networks.
