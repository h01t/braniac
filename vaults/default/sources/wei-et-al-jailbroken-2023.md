# Wei et al., Jailbroken (2023)

**Summary**: A study analyzing how and why safety training fails in large language models, leading them to generate harmful content when given adversarial prompts ("jailbreaking").
**Source Context**: Wei, A., Haghtalab, N., Steinhardt, J. *Jailbroken: How does LLM safety training fail?*, arXiv:2307.02483 (2023). From Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf (citation 177)

---

This paper investigates the failure modes of safety-aligned [[concepts/large-language-models.md]]. The authors likely conduct systematic testing to uncover prompts or scenarios that circumvent the model's safety training, causing it to produce outputs it was trained to suppress.

The work contributes directly to the field of [[concepts/safety-red-teaming.md]] by providing a diagnostic analysis of safety training vulnerabilities. Understanding these failures is critical for developing more robust [[concepts/alignment.md]] techniques.
