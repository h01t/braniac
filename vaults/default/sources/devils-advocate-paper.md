# DEVIL'S ADVOCATE: Anticipatory Reflection for LLM Agents

**Summary**: A paper introducing anticipatory reflection, where agents proactively reflect on potential failures and consider remedies before execution, acting as a "devil's advocate."
**Source Context**: Fundamentals of Building Autonomous LLM Agents.pdf (cited as [53])

---

## Overview
This paper presents a distinct perspective on [[concepts/reflection.md]], emphasizing front-loaded introspection to anticipate and mitigate challenges before action execution (Source: Section 4.3).

## Key Concept
**Anticipatory Reflection**: The agent reflects on potential failures and alternative remedies proactively, enhancing consistency and adaptability in complex tasks (Source: Section 4.3).

## Integration
Used in [[concepts/reasoning-system.md]] examples like DPPM, where agents consider issues during parallel planning (Source: Section 4.4).

## Contrast with Other Work
Differs from post-execution reflection in papers like [[sources/reflection-language-agents-paper.md]], focusing on pre-emptive error handling.
