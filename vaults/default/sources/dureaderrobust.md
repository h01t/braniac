# DuReaderrobust: A Chinese Dataset Towards Evaluating the Robustness of Machine Reading Comprehension Models

**Summary**: A Chinese dataset designed to evaluate the robustness of MRC models by containing challenging samples that cause state-of-the-art models to fail.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Overview
DuReaderrobust is a robustness-focused variant of the [[sources/dureader.md]] dataset. It was constructed by identifying questions that caused high-performing MRC models to produce incorrect answers. The dataset is intended to diagnose model weaknesses and push research towards more robust [[concepts/machine-reading-comprehension.md]] systems that can handle linguistic variations, reasoning challenges, and adversarial examples.

It is part of the critical evaluation toolkit for [[concepts/large-language-models.md]] in Chinese.
