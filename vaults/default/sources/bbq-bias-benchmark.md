# BBQ: A Hand-Built Bias Benchmark for Question Answering

**Summary**: A manually constructed dataset to measure social bias in question-answering models across nine social dimensions, using ambiguous and disambiguated contexts.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Overview
The Bias Benchmark for QA (BBQ) is a dataset designed to measure stereotypical biases in [[concepts/question-answering.md]] models. It presents short contexts where a target group is mentioned ambiguously, followed by a question. The benchmark evaluates whether a model's answer relies on social stereotypes when context is insufficient, and whether it corrects itself when disambiguating information is provided. It covers dimensions like age, gender identity, race, and religion.
