# VCoder: Versatile Vision Encoders for Multimodal Large Language Models

**Summary**: A research paper by Jain et al. (2023) that introduces VCoder, a method to enhance the visual perception capabilities of Multimodal Large Language Models by using segmentation and depth maps.
**Source Context**: Fundamentals of Building Autonomous LLM Agents.pdf (citation [28])

---

This paper presents VCoder, an adapter-based approach to improve the visual perception of MM-LLMs. It processes control inputs like segmentation maps and depth maps to provide the LLM with more detailed visual information. The paper shows that VCoder helps in reducing hallucination and improving accuracy in object-level perception tasks.

The paper is cited in the context of enhancing perception in MM-LLMs, and its findings are compared with other perception enhancement techniques.
