# Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

**Summary**: A comprehensive survey paper providing a detailed overview of Large Language Models, covering their background, architectures, training, fine-tuning, applications, and challenges.
**Source Context**: Self-reference.

---

## Document Details
This is the primary source document for the compiled knowledge. The full title is "A Comprehensive Overview of Large Language Models" (arXiv:2307.06435v10). It was submitted on October 18, 2024, and serves as a review article intended to be a systematic survey and quick reference for researchers and practitioners (Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf).

## Scope and Contents
The paper aims to provide a self-contained, comprehensive overview of the LLM field. It discusses:
*   Relevant background concepts (e.g., [[concepts/tokenization.md]], [[concepts/attention-mechanism.md]]).
*   Core topics like [[concepts/pretraining.md]], [[concepts/llm-architecture.md]], and [[concepts/fine-tuning.md]].
*   Advanced topics at the frontier, including [[concepts/multimodal-llms.md]], [[concepts/augmented-llms.md]], efficiency methods, evaluation, and challenges.
*   Summaries of major models like [[entities/t5-model.md]] and [[entities/gpt-3-model.md]] (Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf).

## Authors and Affiliation
The paper is authored by a large, international team of researchers from institutions including The University of Sydney, University of Engineering and Technology Lahore, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, CSIRO, King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals, and several Australian universities (Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf).
