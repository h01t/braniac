# Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

**Summary**: A survey paper summarizing the landscape of large language models up to 2024, covering architecture, training data, optimization, evaluation, and listing dozens of specific models.
**Source Context**: Primary source document for this knowledge base.

---

This survey paper provides a structured, tabular summary of the field of [[concepts/large-language-models.md]]. It serves as a meta-source, aggregating information from numerous original model publications (e.g., arXiv papers, conference proceedings).

## Content Overview
The document is organized into several key sections with accompanying tables:
*   **Model Summaries**: Tables listing pre-trained and instruction-tuned models >10B parameters, including details on creators, size, training data, hardware, and cost (Tables 3 & 4).
*   **Architecture Details**: A comparative table of architectural choices across models, covering attention type, positional embeddings, activation functions, and layer configurations (Table 5).
*   **Optimization Settings**: Tables detailing hyperparameters like batch size, learning rate schedule, and optimizer for both pre-training and instruction tuning (Tables 6 & 7).
*   **Datasets and Evaluation**: Discusses the importance of training data (Section 5.1, Table 8) and provides an overview of evaluation benchmarks and tasks for [[concepts/natural-language-understanding-nlu.md]] and [[concepts/natural-language-generation-nlg.md]] (Section 5.2).

## Use as a Source
This document is the immediate source for the factual claims in this wiki unless otherwise noted. It compiles information from primary sources, which should be consulted for the most detailed and authoritative information on any specific model.
