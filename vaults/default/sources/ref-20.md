## Content Referenced
This reference supports multiple claims regarding OpenAI's corporate restructuring and governance:
- In October 2025, OpenAI restructured its for-profit entity into the [[entities/openai-group.md]] public benefit corporation (PBC) under the nonprofit [[entities/openai-foundation.md]].
- The [[entities/openai-foundation.md]] retains significant equity (about 26%) and governance control.
- The original capped-profit model was changed to a PBC structure to raise more capital for AGI development.
