# Comprehensive Overview of LLMs

**Summary**: An academic paper or report that provides a detailed survey of Large Language Models, covering their architectures, training objectives, adaptation stages, and specific model implementations.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

This source serves as a foundational survey document on Large Language Models (LLMs). It structures its content to cover the field systematically.

**Key Sections Covered**:
*   **Training Objectives**: Describes [[concepts/training-objectives.md|causal, prefix, masked, and unified language modeling]].
*   **Scaling Laws**: Reviews the empirical research on how model performance relates to scale, citing key studies like Kaplan et al. (2020) and Hoffmann et al. (2022) [[concepts/scaling-laws.md]].
*   **Adaptation Stages**: Outlines the full LLM pipeline from [[concepts/pre-training.md]], through various [[concepts/fine-tuning.md]] methods (transfer learning, instruction-tuning, [[concepts/alignment-tuning.md]]), to final [[concepts/prompting-techniques.md|prompting and utilization]].
*   **Model Catalog**: Provides summaries of numerous specific pre-trained LLMs, including their architectures, key features, and findings (e.g., [[entities/gpt-3.md]], [[entities/t5.md]], [[entities/bloom.md]], [[entities/palm.md]], [[entities/chinchilla.md]]).

The document includes references to many primary research papers and acts as a synthesis of knowledge in the LLM field up to its publication date. It is used as the primary source for the information compiled in this wiki.
