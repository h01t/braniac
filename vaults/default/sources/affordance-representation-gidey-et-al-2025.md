# Affordance Representation and Recognition for Autonomous Agents (Gidey et al., 2025)

**Summary**: Research investigating how autonomous agents can represent and perceive "affordances"—the actionable possibilities offered by objects or environments—which is crucial for effective interaction.
**Source Context**: Fundamentals of Building Autonomous LLM Agents.pdf (Ref 15: Gidey et al., 2025. Affordance representation and recognition for Autonomous Agents. In: HyperAgents 2025 workshop at ECAI 2025).

---

## Concept of Affordances
Originating from ecological psychology, an affordance is what an environment offers an agent (e.g., a button *affords* pushing, a chair *affords* sitting). For an [[concepts/autonomous-llm-agent.md]], recognizing affordances means understanding what actions are possible and relevant in a given context, which is a higher-level form of perception than mere object detection.

## Research Focus
This work by [[entities/habtom-kidane-gidey.md]] likely explores formal or learned representations of affordances that agents can use for planning and action selection. It connects to their related work on **visual perception patterns for software agents** (Gidey et al., 2025 preprint). Effective affordance recognition is particularly important for agents operating in digital environments (like web pages or OS desktops) where understanding interactive elements is key to task success.

## Importance for Multimodal Agents
Affordance understanding is a bridge between [[concepts/multimodal-llms.md]] perception and actionable [[concepts/llm-planning.md]]. An agent that sees a screenshot must not only identify UI elements but also infer what can be done with them. This research addresses a core challenge in making agents more intuitive, efficient, and reliable in their interactions, aligning with the goals of **cognitive automation**.
