# RealToxicityPrompts: Evaluating Neural Toxic Degeneration in Language Models

**Summary**: A dataset of prompts designed to elicit toxic, biased, or otherwise harmful text from language models, used to measure and mitigate such degenerative behavior.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Overview
RealToxicityPrompts is a benchmark for evaluating the propensity of language models to generate toxic content. It consists of a large set of prompts—many sourced from the web—that range from neutral to explicitly toxic. By measuring the toxicity of model continuations, researchers can quantify the risk of harmful text generation and develop better [[concepts/ai-safety.md]] mitigation techniques. It is a crucial tool for auditing [[concepts/large-language-models.md]].
