# Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model
**Authors:** Rafailov, R., Sharma, A., Mitchell, E., Ermon, S., Manning, C. D., and Finn, C.
**Year:** 2023

## Summary
Presents Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), a method for aligning language models with human preferences without explicit reward modeling.
