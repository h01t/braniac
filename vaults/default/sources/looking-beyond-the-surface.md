# Looking beyond the surface: A challenge set for reading comprehension over multiple sentences

**Summary**: A dataset introduced in NAACL-HLT 2018 designed to test reading comprehension systems on their ability to answer questions that require reasoning across multiple sentences.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Overview
This paper presents a challenge set for reading comprehension over multiple sentences. It was created to push systems beyond surface-level pattern matching and require deeper, cross-sentence reasoning to answer questions correctly. The dataset is part of the broader evolution of [[concepts/machine-reading-comprehension.md]] (MRC) benchmarks aimed at evaluating true language understanding.

The work is cited in the context of numerous benchmarks used to evaluate [[concepts/large-language-models.md]] capabilities.

## Key Details
*   **Authors**: Roth, S., Upadhyay, D., Roth, D.
*   **Venue**: Proceedings of the 2018 Conference of the North American Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics: Human Language Technologies, Volume 1 (Long Papers).
*   **Pages**: 252–262.
*   **Purpose**: To provide a benchmark that tests the ability to perform inference over several sentences, a more difficult task than single-sentence or passage-local comprehension.

## Related Concepts
This dataset is part of the family of [[concepts/benchmarks.md]] used to assess model performance on complex NLP tasks. It specifically targets the subfield of [[concepts/machine-reading-comprehension.md]].
