# OpenAI Non-Profit Era (2015-2018)

**Summary**: The initial period of OpenAI's existence from its 2015 founding through 2018, during which it operated as a pure non-profit research laboratory focused on open and safe AI development.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

OpenAI was founded in December 2015 as OpenAI, Inc., a 501(c)(3) non-profit research company. Its stated mission was to advance digital intelligence in a way that is most likely to benefit humanity as a whole, unconstrained by a need to generate financial return.

## Founding Motivations and Early Work
The founding group, which included Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and Ilya Sutskever, was motivated by concerns about the potential existential risks of [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence.md]] and a desire to counterbalance the concentration of AI power in large corporations. Early research (2016-2018) focused on fundamental reinforcement learning, robotics, and generative models. A key principle was a strong commitment to openness, publishing most research papers and releasing open-source software, such as the Gym toolkit for reinforcement learning.

## Transition to Capped-Profit
The non-profit model faced challenges due to the escalating costs of AI research, particularly the compute required for training large language models. By 2018, the financial demands of competing with companies like Google were becoming unsustainable. This led directly to the 2019 reorganization into the [[concepts/openai-capped-profit-structure.md]], marking the end of its pure non-profit phase.
