# Chinese Open-Source LLM Landscape (2025)

**Summary**: By mid-2025, China had become a global leader in the number of publicly released [[concepts/large-language-model.md]]s, driven by heavy state and industry investment, permissive open licensing, and a strategic pivot by tech giants and startups toward publicly shared models.
**Source Context**: https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/chinese-open-source-llms-2025

---

## Scale and Context
According to Chinese state media cited by Reuters, by July 2025 China accounted for **1,509** of the world’s roughly 3,755 publicly released LLMs, far more than any other country [^1]. This represents a "revival" of [[concepts/open-source-ai.md]], with dozens of Chinese LLMs available for download or use via platforms like Hugging Face, GitHub, or cloud APIs [^1][^2].

## Key Characteristics
The ecosystem features both **general-purpose foundation models** with parameters ranging from billions to hundreds of billions, and **specialized chatbots and domain experts**. Many are built on advanced architectures like [[concepts/mixture-of-experts.md]] (MoE) or feature ultra-long context windows [^1].

## Competitive Dynamics
Competition is fierce, with Chinese announcements often highlighting that their open models match or exceed the performance of leading U.S. models on various benchmarks. For example, Alibaba claimed its Qwen-2.5-Max outperformed [[entities/deepseek.md]]'s V3 model, and Reuters reported that Alibaba's Qwen3-Coder rivaled OpenAI's GPT-4 on code tasks [^4][^7]. The landscape includes offerings from major tech firms (e.g., [[entities/alibaba-cloud.md]], [[entities/baidu.md]], [[entities/bytedance-moonshot-ai.md]]), leading startups ([[entities/deepseek.md]], [[entities/zhipu-ai.md]], [[entities/baichuan-ai.md]], [[entities/minimax.md]]), and academic labs [^1].

## Driving Forces
This explosion is attributed to heavy state and industry investment in domestic AI, the adoption of permissive open licenses (often Apache- or MIT-style), and a strategic shift by Chinese tech companies toward open-sourcing their models to foster ecosystem growth and technological credibility [^1][^2][^11].
