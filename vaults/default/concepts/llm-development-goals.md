# LLM Development Goals

**Summary**: This article aims to be a comprehensive resource for researchers, providing fundamental concepts and recent advancements to guide the development of better Large Language Models (LLMs).
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Purpose and Scope
The stated purpose of this source material is to offer insights into recent advancements in LLMs and provide the fundamental concepts and details necessary for developing better models [[concepts/llm-advancements.md]]. It is positioned as a valuable resource for researchers in the field.

## Key Development Areas
While the summary is broad, the extensive reference list points to major areas of focus for modern LLM development. These include scaling model size and data [[concepts/scaling-laws.md]], novel pre-training architectures [[concepts/transformer-architecture.md]], instruction following and alignment techniques [[concepts/instruction-tuning.md]], and improving efficiency via model compression [[concepts/model-compression.md]].

The acknowledgment of support from the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) and King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM) indicates this work is part of a concerted research effort in artificial intelligence.
