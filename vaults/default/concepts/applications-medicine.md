# Applications of LLMs in Medicine

**Summary**: Large Language Models (LLMs) are being integrated into various aspects of healthcare, from clinical decision support and patient interaction to medical research, education, and public health initiatives.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Clinical Decision Support
LLMs analyze patient data and medical literature to assist physicians. They can help identify potential diagnoses, suggest appropriate tests, and recommend evidence-based treatment strategies [435, 436, 437, 438]. This acts as an advanced form of [[concepts/retrieval-augmented-generation.md]].

## Patient Interaction and Chatbots
LLMs power chatbot applications that answer patient queries about symptoms or medications, schedule appointments, and provide essential health advice [439, 440, 441]. This enhances accessibility but also raises concerns about [[concepts/hallucinations.md]] and [[concepts/safety-controllability.md]].

## Medical Research
LLMs expedite research by extracting and filtering information from vast medical literature. They can identify relevant studies, summarize findings, and even predict future research trends [442, 443, 444].

## Medical Education
For education, LLMs help create training materials, generate exam questions, explain complex topics, and provide personalized feedback to students [445, 446, 447, 448]. They can also simulate patient interactions for clinical skills practice.

## Public Health
LLMs support public health by analyzing media data to detect disease outbreaks, monitor public sentiment towards health policies, and disseminate clear health information [449]. However, their use must address issues like data privacy and the propagation of [[concepts/bias-fairness.md]] [450, 451].
