# AI Safety and Alignment

**Summary**: The field of research focused on ensuring that advanced artificial intelligence systems are robust, controllable, and act in accordance with human values and intentions.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI (mentioned under "AI Safety, Alignment, and Risk Management")

---

AI safety and alignment addresses the technical challenge of building AI that is not just capable, but also reliably beneficial. For powerful systems like [[concepts/gpt-models.md]], misalignment could lead to unintended harmful behaviors, even if no malice is intended.

## Core Problems and Approaches
Key research problems include: **Specification** (correctly defining the objective for the AI), **Robustness** (ensuring the AI performs well under novel conditions or adversarial attacks), and **Control** (maintaining the ability to oversee or shut down a system). Techniques involve reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), scalable oversight, and interpretability research. For [[entities/openai.md]], safety rhetoric is central to its brand, but its practices have been scrutinized, especially during events like the 2023 [[concepts/openai-leadership-instability.md]] where board concerns about safety were cited.
