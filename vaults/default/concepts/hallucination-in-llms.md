# Hallucination in LLMs

**Summary**: The phenomenon where language models generate factually incorrect or nonsensical information.

## Causes
- Over-reliance on training data patterns.
- Lack of grounding in factual knowledge.
- Confabulation in open-ended generation.

## Mitigation
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- Chain-of-Thought verification.
- Human oversight.
