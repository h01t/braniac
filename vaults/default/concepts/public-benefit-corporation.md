# Public Benefit Corporation (PBC)

**Summary**: A type of for-profit corporate entity legally required to consider the impact of its decisions not only on shareholders but also on society and the environment. OpenAI is structured as a PBC.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## OpenAI's Structure
[[entities/openai.md]] is a private public benefit corporation (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI). This legal structure aligns with its founding mission to develop [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence.md]] (AGI) for the benefit of humanity, requiring it to balance profit motives with its stated public benefit purpose.
