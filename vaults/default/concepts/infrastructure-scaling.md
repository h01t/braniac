# Infrastructure Scaling

**Summary**: The large-scale expansion of computing infrastructure, including data centers and hardware partnerships, to support advanced AI model training and inference.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## OpenAI's Efforts
OpenAI's primary infrastructure initiative is the [[entities/stargate-project.md]], a joint venture targeting up to 10 gigawatts of data center capacity [[sources/ref-54.md]].
The company formed hardware partnerships with [[entities/nvidia.md]], [[entities/amd.md]], and [[entities/broadcom.md]] to secure AI accelerators and GPUs [[sources/ref-58-62.md]].
Analysts projected $400 billion in infrastructure funding needs over 12 months and $50-60 billion annually for capacity exceeding two gigawatts by late 2025 [[sources/ref-63-64.md]].

## Hybrid Approach
OpenAI employs a hybrid infrastructure approach, maintaining ties with [[entities/microsoft-azure.md]] while expanding multi-cloud and owned infrastructure via Stargate for scalability and resilience.
