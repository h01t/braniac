# Google Discrimination Settlements

**Summary**: A series of major legal settlements where Google paid hundreds of millions of dollars to resolve class-action lawsuits alleging systemic gender and racial discrimination in pay, promotion, and workplace culture.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

Google has faced multiple high-profile lawsuits alleging patterns of discrimination, leading to significant financial settlements.

**Gender Discrimination**
*   **2022 Settlement**: Google agreed to pay $118 million to settle a class-action lawsuit involving over 15,500 female employees. The suit alleged that from 2013 to 2017, Google systemically paid women less than men in comparable roles and unfairly passed them over for promotions in California.
*   **2023 Jury Award**: A separate case resulted in a jury awarding $1.15 million to a female executive who successfully sued for gender discrimination and retaliation.

**Racial Discrimination**
*   **2025 Settlement**: Google agreed to a $50 million settlement to resolve a class-action lawsuit brought by Black employees. The suit claimed systemic bias against Black workers in hiring, advancement, and workplace culture at Google.

**Company Response**
While settling these cases, Google did not admit to wrongdoing. In response to such lawsuits and internal pressure, the company has implemented measures like diversity training, pay equity audits, and revised hiring practices. These settlements occur against a backdrop of ongoing scrutiny of [[concepts/diversity-and-inclusion.md]] results in the tech industry.
