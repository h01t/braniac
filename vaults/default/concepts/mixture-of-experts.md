# Mixture of Experts (MoE)

**Summary**: An architecture that divides the model into multiple "expert" subnetworks, with a gating mechanism that activates only a subset of experts for each input.

## Benefits
- Increased model capacity without proportional compute cost.
- Enables scaling to trillions of parameters.
