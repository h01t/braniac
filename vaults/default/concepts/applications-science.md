# Applications of LLMs in Science

**Summary**: LLMs accelerate scientific research by summarizing literature, aiding in hypothesis generation, and assisting with scientific writing and communication.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Literature Review and Summarization
LLMs can quickly analyze and summarize vast amounts of scientific literature, helping researchers stay up-to-date with findings even outside their core expertise [456, 457].

## Hypothesis and Research Question Generation
Their ability to process large-scale datasets allows LLMs to uncover insights and patterns not immediately apparent to human researchers, aiding in the formulation of new hypotheses and research questions [458].

## Scientific Writing
LLMs assist researchers in drafting documents, suggesting improvements, and ensuring adherence to specific formatting guidelines, which saves time and improves the clarity of scientific communication [459, 460].
