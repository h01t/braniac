# Antitrust Actions

**Summary**: Legal actions by government authorities to prevent monopolistic practices, collusion, or anti-competitive behavior in the AI industry.

## Context
As AI becomes dominated by a few large companies, regulators have investigated partnerships, pricing, and data access.

## Examples
- Regulatory scrutiny of [[entities/microsoft]]'s investment in OpenAI.
- Investigations into alleged collusion in the AI chip market.
