# OpenAI Partnerships

**Summary**: Details OpenAI's strategic commercial relationships with major technology firms, consultancies, and media companies to secure compute, funding, and enterprise integration, while noting a critical dependence on Microsoft.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Microsoft Strategic Partnership
OpenAI's partnership with Microsoft is a cornerstone of its operations, providing critical financing and compute infrastructure for projects like the [[concepts/stargate-project.md]] [Source]. In March 2026, OpenAI disclosed in an investor document that Microsoft supplies "a substantial portion of our financing and compute," identifying this reliance as a significant business risk [Source]. The company warned that modifying or terminating this partnership could adversely affect its business, though it reaffirmed Microsoft as a "critical long-term partner" [Source]. Microsoft holds a diluted but significant stake of approximately 27% in OpenAI [Source]. This partnership also drove significant growth for Microsoft's Azure cloud platform, contributing to $51.5 billion in cloud revenue for Q2 FY2026 [Source].

## 2025 Enterprise Partnerships
In 2025, OpenAI announced several high-profile enterprise partnerships to embed its models into business applications, with many featured at [[concepts/devday.md|DevDay 2025]] [Source]. These initiatives aimed to expand beyond consumer use into developer tools and integrations.
*   **Salesforce**: Partnership for AI-enhanced Customer Relationship Management (CRM) announced October 14 [Source].
*   **Samsung**: A hardware-software alliance announced October 1 [Source].
*   **Disney**: A $1 billion deal announced December 11, licensing over 200 characters from Disney, Marvel, Star Wars, and Pixar to boost the [[concepts/sora.md|Sora]] video generation model [Source].
*   **Other Integrations**: Partnerships were also formed with Spotify and Zillow [Source].

## 2026 Enterprise Expansion
OpenAI intensified its enterprise focus in early 2026 with major deals.
*   **Snowflake**: On February 2, 2026, a multi-year, $200 million agreement was announced to embed OpenAI's frontier intelligence into Snowflake's data platform, including Snowflake Cortex AI [Source].
*   **Consulting "Frontier Alliances"**: On February 23, 2026, OpenAI formed multiyear alliances with consulting firms Accenture, Boston Consulting Group, Capgemini, and McKinsey & Co. to help deploy its Frontier AI agent platform within enterprises [Source].
*   **Walmart**: An earlier partnership in October 2025 with Walmart created AI-first shopping experiences via ChatGPT with Instant Checkout [Source].

## Graphic Design Ecosystem Integrations
OpenAI's generative models, via API, are integrated into established design platforms to enhance creative workflows. OpenAI does not offer its own dedicated design software.
*   **Canva's Magic Studio**: Pairs OpenAI's API with Canva's design engine and asset library, used billions of times for generating presentations and social media content [Source].
*   **Adobe Express**: Integrated with ChatGPT to allow users to create marketing assets from professional templates [Source].

## Hardware & Infrastructure Partnerships
To support massive compute scaling, particularly for the Stargate project, OpenAI formed unprecedented hardware procurement agreements in late 2025. These deals, exceeding $1 trillion in aggregate value, are intended to enable independent scaling beyond providers like Microsoft Azure [Source].
*   **NVIDIA**: A September 22, 2025 letter of intent for 10 gigawatts of systems (millions of GPUs), initially valued up to $100 billion [Source]. This was followed by a $30 billion investment from NVIDIA in OpenAI's $110 billion funding round in February 2026 [Source].
*   **AMD**: A multi-year deal on October 6, 2025, for 6 gigawatts of Instinct GPUs, starting with 1 gigawatt in 2026 [Source].
*   **Broadcom**: A letter of intent on October 13, 2025, for 10 gigawatts of custom accelerators [Source].
