# Aha Moment
An intriguing phenomenon observed during the RL training of [[entities/DeepSeek-R1-Zero.md]] where an intermediate model version spontaneously learns to allocate more thinking time to a problem by **reevaluating its initial approach**. This demonstrates the model's growing metacognitive reasoning abilities.

This moment exemplifies the power of [[concepts/reinforcement_learning.md]]: by providing the right incentives, the model autonomously develops advanced problem-solving strategies without explicit instruction.
