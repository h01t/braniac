# Open-Source AI

**Summary**: Open-source AI refers to artificial intelligence technologies, particularly software and model weights, that are made publicly available under permissive licenses, allowing for use, modification, and distribution by anyone.
**Source Context**: https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/chinese-open-source-llms-2025

---

## Key Characteristics
In the context of [[concepts/large-language-model.md]]s, open-source typically means releasing the model's architecture (code), trained parameters (weights), and sometimes training data under licenses like Apache 2.0 or MIT. This fosters collaboration, accelerates research, lowers barriers to entry, and allows for community auditing and improvement.

## Role in the Chinese LLM Landscape
The article highlights that a "revival" of open-source AI is a key feature of the [[concepts/chinese-open-source-llm-landscape-2025.md]]. Heavy investment and a strategic pivot by Chinese tech giants and startups toward open licensing (Apache- or MIT-style) have resulted in a vast ecosystem of publicly available models [^1]. Companies like [[entities/deepseek.md]] and [[entities/minimax.md]] emphasize the low-cost and transparent nature of their open-source models as a competitive advantage [^11][^23].

## Strategic Motivations
Open-sourcing AI models can serve multiple strategic goals: building developer ecosystems, establishing technological credibility, influencing standards, and complying with or supporting national industrial policy objectives, as seen with companies like [[entities/zhipu-ai.md]] [^5][^11].
