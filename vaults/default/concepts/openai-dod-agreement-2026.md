# OpenAI Department of Defense Agreement (2026)

**Summary**: A reported 2026 partnership agreement between OpenAI and the U.S. Department of Defense, which sparked significant public backlash and debate about the company's commitment to its original "beneficial AI" charter.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

In 2026, OpenAI entered into an agreement with the [[entities/us-department-of-defense.md]], a move that represented a significant shift in its policy on military applications and triggered controversy.

## Details and Context
While specific contract details are not fully public, reports indicated the agreement involved the provision of AI software and expertise for Department of Defense projects. This marked a departure from OpenAI's earlier public stance of not permitting its AI to be used for "military and warfare" purposes. The company reportedly revised its usage policies to allow certain national security-related work, excluding direct weapons development.

## Backlash and Debate
The agreement faced immediate criticism from elements of the AI safety community, ethicists, and some of OpenAI's own user base. Critics argued that partnering with a military institution directly contradicted the founding mission of ensuring AI benefits "all of humanity" and could accelerate an AI arms race. Supporters framed it as a necessary step for U.S. national security in the face of geopolitical competition, particularly with China. The controversy is an example of the real-world ethical and [[concepts/ai-policy-governance.md]] challenges that emerge as powerful AI models transition from research to deployment.
