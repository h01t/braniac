# AI-Native Organization

**Summary**: An organization whose processes, workflows, and iteration cycles are fundamentally redesigned and accelerated through the deep integration of autonomous AI agents.
**Source Context**: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en

---

## Definition
An AI-native organization is one that leverages advanced AI not just as a tool, but as an integral component of its core operational loop. The source states that the capabilities of models like [[entities/minimax-m27.md|MiniMax M2.7]] are "significantly accelerating our own evolution into an AI-native organization" (Source). This involves using AI to automate and enhance internal development, research, and decision-making processes.

## MiniMax's Internal Workflow
The article provides a concrete example: the use of an AI-powered [[concepts/agent-harness.md|research agent harness]] within MiniMax. This harness interacts with research teams, automates experiment setup, monitoring, and analysis, and drives the model iteration cycle (Source).

The key shift is that "human researchers only interact for critical decisions and discussions," while the AI handles a significant portion of the workflow. This structure "accelerates problem discovery and experimentation, delivering models faster" (Source).

## Foundation in Self-Evolution
The transition to an AI-native organization is tightly linked to the concept of [[concepts/self-evolution-in-ai.md|AI self-evolution]]. When models can participate in optimizing their own development pipelines and the tools used to build them, the organization's pace of innovation can increase dramatically. The source describes this as initiating "a cycle of model self-evolution" that also transforms the organization (Source).
