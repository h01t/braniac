# Content Moderation

**Summary**: The process of monitoring and removing harmful or policy-violating content generated or amplified by AI systems.

## Techniques
- **Keyword Filtering**: Blocking specific words/phrases.
- **Classifiers**: ML models that identify hate speech, violence, etc.
- **Human Review**: Human-in-the-loop for edge cases.

## Relation to AI Safety
