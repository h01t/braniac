# AI Ethics

**Summary**: The branch of ethics concerned with the development and deployment of artificial intelligence systems, focusing on fairness, accountability, transparency, and human rights.

## Key Principles
- **Fairness**: Avoiding bias in training data and model outputs.
- **Accountability**: Establishing responsibility for AI-driven decisions.
- **Transparency**: Making model behavior interpretable and explainable.
- **Privacy**: Protecting user data in AI systems.
