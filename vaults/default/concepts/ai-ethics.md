# AI Ethics

**Summary**: The study of moral issues and normative frameworks applied to the design, development, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence technologies.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI (implied in sections on controversies, content moderation, and societal impact)

---

AI ethics is an interdisciplinary field that examines how AI systems interact with human values, rights, and social structures. It moves beyond technical safety to consider broader societal consequences, fairness, and justice.

## Key Ethical Concerns in AI Deployment
Prominent issues include: **Bias and Discrimination** (AI perpetuating or amplifying societal prejudices), **Privacy** (erosion through data collection and inference), **Accountability and Transparency** ("black box" models making consequential decisions), **Autonomy and Manipulation** (AI influencing human behavior), **Labor and Economic Justice** (impact of automation), and **Dual-Use and Militarization** (e.g., concerns raised by the [[concepts/openai-dod-agreement-2026.md]]). Companies like [[entities/openai.md]] face constant ethical scrutiny regarding their [[concepts/ai-intellectual-property-disputes.md]], content moderation policies, and the real-world harms caused by their products, as noted in reports of suicides and backlash related to AI interactions.
