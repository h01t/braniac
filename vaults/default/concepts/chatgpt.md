# ChatGPT

**Summary**: An AI-powered conversational agent developed by OpenAI, launched in 2022, which quickly became one of the world's most popular consumer applications.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

**ChatGPT** is an AI chatbot developed by [[entities/openai.md]] and launched on November 30, 2022. It is powered by language models from the [[concepts/gpt-series.md]], initially based on GPT-3.5 (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

ChatGPT experienced unprecedented viral growth, reaching 1 million users within five days and 100 million monthly active users by January 2023, setting a record for the fastest-growing consumer application at the time. By March 2026, it reported over 900 million weekly active users (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

The launch of ChatGPT was a pivotal moment for OpenAI, driving massive revenue growth and necessitating a significant infrastructure buildout on [[entities/microsoft.md]] Azure to handle demand. It also served as the primary interface for users to access later models like [[concepts/gpt-4o.md]] and the [[concepts/o1-series.md]] (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
