# AI in Graphic Design

**Summary**: The use of artificial intelligence tools to assist or automate the creation of visual designs, logos, layouts, and illustrations.

## Tools and Models
- **Text-to-Image Generation**: Model families like [[concepts/dall-e.md]] and others.
- **Style Transfer**: Applying the style of one image to another.
- **Layout Generation**: AI-powered tools for arranging design elements.

## Impact
AI reduces the time and cost of design iterations, enabling rapid prototyping and personalized visual content.
