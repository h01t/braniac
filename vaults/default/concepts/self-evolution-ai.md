# Self-Evolution in AI
- **Definition**: The ability of AI models to actively participate in and accelerate their own development cycles, reducing human intervention.
- **Mechanism**: Involves iterative processes such as analyzing failure trajectories, planning changes, modifying code, running evaluations, and making decisions based on feedback.
- **Example in M2.7**: MiniMax M2.7 can build [[concepts/agent-harness.md]], update memory, and optimize learning processes autonomously, achieving performance improvements like 30% on internal evaluations.
- **Benchmark**: Tested using MLE Bench Lite, where M2.7 showed continuous improvement over time.
- **Significance**: Enables faster model iterations and paves the way for fully autonomous AI evolution.
