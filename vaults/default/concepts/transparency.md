# Transparency

**Summary**: The degree of openness in an organization's operations, model development, and decision-making, a key area of criticism for OpenAI.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

OpenAI has faced sustained criticism for limited transparency, despite public commitments to disclosure. In August 2025, over 100 signatories, including Nobel laureates and former employees, issued an open letter urging greater openness about the company's restructuring and adherence to its nonprofit roots. They claimed opacity impedes public evaluation of its legal duties.

Specific issues include:
*   **Leaked Documents**: Revealed shifts in profit prioritization, with return caps rising and possible elimination by 2025, alongside undisclosed safety issues.
*   **Delayed Disclosure**: A 2023 employee credential breach was notified internally in April but public disclosure was delayed.
*   **Closed Models**: The shift from an open-source approach to closed frontier models (citing misuse risks) has been criticized for undermining trust and innovation. Models like [[entities/o1.md]] omit internal reasoning.
*   **Benchmarking Conflicts**: Accusations of undue influence, such as funding the "independent" FrontierMath dataset before [[entities/o3.md]] set records.

OpenAI's responses have included the May 2025 Safety Evaluations Hub and system cards for models. However, the lack of a safety report for GPT-4.1 and the indefinite postponement of open-source releases illustrate ongoing tensions between safety, competition, and transparency.
