# Anatomy Concepts
Topics from anatomy exams, covering structures of the human body.

## Examples from Source
- Parts of the parietal pleura: excludes visceral pleura.
- Structures of the sphenoid bone: includes sella turcica, foramen spinosum, etc.
- Right atrium structures: includes pectinate muscles.
- Pharynx divisions: includes nasopharynx, oropharynx, laryngopharynx.
- Location of hypoglossal nucleus: in the medulla oblongata.
- Cranial nerves exiting from the dorsal side of the brainstem: trochlear nerve.
