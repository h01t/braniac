# Applications of LLMs in Mathematics

**Summary**: LLMs assist in solving mathematical problems, verifying proofs, and enhancing the accessibility of complex mathematical concepts for specialists and non-specialists alike.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Problem Solving and Explanation
LLMs can solve mathematical problems by providing step-by-step explanations and guiding users through complex proofs and calculations. They can also identify errors in reasoning or computation and suggest corrections [461, 462].

## Proof Verification
LLMs can be employed to check the validity of mathematical proofs, serving as a preliminary filter before human review, though they are not a substitute for meticulous mathematical work [463, 464].

## Accessibility and Translation
LLMs enhance accessibility by translating complex mathematical concepts and findings into understandable language for non-specialists, helping to bridge the gap between theoretical mathematics and applied contexts [465].
