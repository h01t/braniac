# Reasoning Benchmarks
Standardized evaluations used to measure the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs). The benchmarks cited assess performance in mathematics, coding, and graduate-level QA.

**Key Benchmarks:**
*   **AIME 2024 (American Invitational Mathematics Examination):** A challenging math competition problem set.
*   **MATH-500:** A dataset of 500 challenging math problems.
*   **GPQA (Graduate-Level Google-Proof Q&A):** A diamond-tier benchmark of graduate-level questions across multiple domains.
*   **LiveCodeBench:** A holistic and contamination-free evaluation for code generation.
*   **SWE-bench:** A benchmark for evaluating software engineering capabilities (mentioned in future work).

**Usage in Evaluation:**
These benchmarks were used to compare [[entities/DeepSeek-R1.md]], its distilled variants (e.g., [[entities/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B.md]]), and competitor models like [[entities/OpenAI-o1-mini.md]] and [[entities/QwQ-32B-Preview.md]].
