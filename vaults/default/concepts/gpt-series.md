# GPT Series

**Summary**: The family of transformer-based large language models developed by OpenAI, including GPT-3, GPT-4, GPT-4o, and GPT-5.

## Evolution
- **GPT-3**: Introduced in 2020, 175B parameters.
- **GPT-4**: Multimodal, 2023.
- **GPT-4o**: Optimized for real-time interaction.
- **GPT-5**: Latest generation (2025/2026).
