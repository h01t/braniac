# Professional Work Applications of AI
- **Overview**: Use of AI models in office and professional settings for tasks such as document editing, financial analysis, and research report generation.
- **Capabilities of M2.7**:
  - **Office Suite**: Enhanced ability for complex editing in Excel, PPT, and Word, with support for multi-round revisions and high-fidelity editing.
  - **Financial Modeling**: Can autonomously read annual reports, design assumptions, build revenue models (e.g., for TSMC), and produce deliverables like PPTs and Word reports.
  - **Tool Invocation**: High accuracy on benchmarks like Toolathon (46.3%) and MM Claw (62.7%).
- **Benchmarks**: GDPval-AA measures domain expertise and task delivery; M2.7 has ELO 1495, among the highest for open-source models.
- **Significance**: Enables AI to assist in professional workflows, reducing manual effort and improving efficiency.
