# Artificial Intelligence

**Summary**: The broad field of computer science focused on creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

## Subfields
- **Machine Learning** (including [[concepts/large-language-model]]s)
- **Computer Vision**
- **Natural Language Processing**
- **Robotics**

## History
AI has evolved through cycles of optimism and "winters" since the 1950s. Recent advances are driven by deep learning and transformer architectures.
