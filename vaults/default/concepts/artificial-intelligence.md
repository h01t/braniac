# Artificial Intelligence

**Summary**: Artificial Intelligence at Google encompasses a broad range of research, development, and application of machine learning and AI technologies across its products, from search and advertising to autonomous systems and scientific research.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

[[entities/google-llc.md|Google]] has been a pioneer in integrating [[concepts/artificial-intelligence.md]] into consumer and enterprise technologies. Its AI journey includes early machine learning applications, foundational research, and major acquisitions like [[entities/deepmind.md|DeepMind]].

## Key Developments and Timeline
*   **Early 2000s**: Machine learning used for search spelling correction and Google Translate.
*   **2011**: Launch of the Google Brain project to develop deep neural networks.
*   **2014**: Acquisition of DeepMind for over $500 million (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).
*   **2016**: DeepMind's AlphaGo defeats world champion Lee Sedol.
*   **2017**: Introduction of the Transformer neural network architecture, which became foundational for modern large language models.
*   **2018**: Release of the BERT model for natural language understanding.
*   **2020**: AlphaFold makes a breakthrough in predicting protein structures.
*   **2023**: Merging of Google Brain and DeepMind into "Google DeepMind" and the launch of the multimodal Gemini model family (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).
*   **2024-2025**: Gemini models power new features in Search (AI Mode), Google Home, and advance scientific applications in fields like weather prediction and fusion energy research (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).

## Integration and Impact
AI is deeply integrated into Google's core products, enhancing [[concepts/search-engine.md]] ranking (via RankBrain, BERT), optimizing the [[concepts/advertising-ecosystem.md]], improving translation and speech recognition, and enabling new hardware capabilities. It represents a central pillar of the company's future strategy.
