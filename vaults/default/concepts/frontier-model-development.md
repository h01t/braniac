# Frontier Model Development

**Summary**: The process of creating and releasing the most advanced generation of AI models, a core activity for OpenAI involving significant compute resources.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## OpenAI's Model Releases
From 2025-2026, OpenAI released a series of advanced models, including the o3 series, [[entities/gpt-5.md]], and its subsequent iterations [[sources/ref-66-72.md]].
In March 2026, CEO [[entities/sam-altman.md]] announced the completion of pre-training for a new frontier model codenamed "Spud," described as a "very strong model" expected to accelerate economic productivity.

## Resource Prioritization
To prioritize compute resources for frontier model development, OpenAI wound down its [[entities/sora.md]] AI video generation mobile app and API in March 2026.
