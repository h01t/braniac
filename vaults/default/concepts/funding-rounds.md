# Funding Rounds

**Summary**: A chronology of major capital raises by OpenAI, detailing investors, amounts, and valuations from its founding through early 2026, culminating in a record-breaking $110+ billion round.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Chronology of Major Rounds
*   **2015**: $1 billion pledge, yielding $130 million.
*   **2019**: Microsoft investment of $1 billion.
*   **2021**: Microsoft investment of $2 billion.
*   **2023**:
    *   Microsoft investment of $10 billion.
    *   Andreessen Horowitz participated in a $300 million Series E round (April 28, 2023).
    *   Late-2023 valuation reached $90–100 billion.
*   **2024**:
    *   A $6.6 billion round.
    *   A secondary tender offer completed in November 2024. Both were at a $157 billion valuation.
*   **2025**:
    *   Andreessen Horowitz participated in a $40 billion Series F round (March 31, 2025).
    *   An October 2025 $6.6 billion share sale at a $500 billion valuation.
    *   In December 2025, Thrive Capital invested ~$1 billion at a $285 billion valuation via a preferential call option.
*   **Late 2025**: [[entities/softbank.md]] completed a $41 billion investment commitment ($7.5B initial in April 2025, plus $22.5B additional in late December).
*   **February 27, 2026**: Announced a $110 billion funding round at a $730 billion pre-money valuation, resulting in an ~$850 billion post-money valuation. Major investors included:
    *   [[entities/amazon.md]]: $50 billion ($15B initial, $35B contingent).
    *   [[entities/nvidia.md]]: $30 billion.
    *   [[entities/softbank.md]]: $30 billion follow-on investment via SoftBank Vision Fund 2, in three $10B tranches.
*   **Late March 2026**: Raised an additional $10 billion, bringing the total for this round to over $120 billion, as confirmed by CFO Sarah Friar.

## Total Funding
As of March 1, 2026, OpenAI had raised a total of approximately $168-174 billion in funding across all rounds (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
