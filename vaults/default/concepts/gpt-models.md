# GPT Models

**Summary**: The Generative Pre-trained Transformer series developed by OpenAI, a lineage of large language models that form the core of its product offerings and have significantly advanced the field of natural language processing.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

The GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) series is a family of neural network models based on the Transformer architecture. They are pre-trained on vast corpora of text data to predict the next token in a sequence, which gives them broad capabilities in understanding and generating human-like text.

## Evolution and Key Releases
The series began with GPT-1, demonstrating the potential of the transformer-based pre-training approach. GPT-2, released in 2019, was notably larger and demonstrated impressive text generation, leading to a staged release due to concerns about misuse. GPT-3 (2020) was a massive leap in scale (175 billion parameters) and demonstrated few-shot learning, enabling a wide range of applications through its API. Subsequent iterative improvements led to models like GPT-3.5 (which powers the initial release of ChatGPT) and GPT-4, a multimodal model capable of processing both text and image inputs.

## Model Categories and Availability
As of March 2026, OpenAI categorizes its models into three main types (source: Grokipedia/OpenAI):
1.  **Proprietary Models**: Closed-source, available only via API or in ChatGPT (e.g., GPT-4 class models).
2.  **Open-Weight Models**: Model weights are publicly released under permissive licenses like Apache 2.0 (e.g., some GPT-3 variants).
3.  **Historically Open-Source Models**: Earlier models from the company's more open non-profit phase.

These models are the foundational technology behind [[entities/openai.md]]'s flagship products, including ChatGPT and its developer API ecosystem.
