# GPT-3

**Summary**: A large language model released by OpenAI in 2020, notable for its scale of 175 billion parameters and its ability to perform a wide variety of natural language tasks.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

**GPT-3** (Generative Pre-trained Transformer 3) is a large language model released by [[entities/openai.md]] in June 2020. It was a significant leap in scale from its predecessor GPT-2, containing 175 billion parameters. The model was trained on [[entities/microsoft.md]] Azure supercomputing infrastructure (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

GPT-3 demonstrated advanced capabilities in text generation, translation, question-answering, and coding. It was made commercially available via an API, marking a key step in OpenAI's commercialization strategy and enabling developers to build applications on top of it. GPT-3's capabilities were a foundation for the later development of [[concepts/chatgpt.md]] (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
