# Multimodal LLMs

**Summary**: Language models that can process and generate multiple modalities (text, images, audio, video).

## Examples
- GPT-4V, Claude 3, Gemini, Kimi K2.5.

## Training Approaches
- **Early Fusion**: Combine modalities from the start (e.g., [[concepts/early-fusion-multimodal-pre-training.md]]).
- **Late Fusion**: Align separate encoders and decoders.
