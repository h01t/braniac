# Multimodal LLMs (MLLMs)

**Summary**: Models that extend large language models to process and integrate multiple input modalities such as images, video, and audio, enabling richer, more context-aware interactions.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

Multimodal LLMs (MLLMs) are designed to perceive and process information beyond text, incorporating modalities like images, video, and audio [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf]. This integration allows for a deeper understanding of context and more intelligent, expressive responses that align with human multisensory perception. MLLMs support intuitive and flexible user interfaces.

Current approaches to constructing MLLMs are generally divided into three streams: [[concepts/pre-training-for-mllms.md]], [[concepts/fine-tuning-for-mllms.md]], and [[concepts/prompting-for-mllms.md]] [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf]. A key application area for these models is [[concepts/visual-reasoning-with-llms.md]].
