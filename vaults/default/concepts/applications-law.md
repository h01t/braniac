# Applications of LLMs in Law

**Summary**: LLMs assist in legal research by analyzing documents, generating explanations of legal terms, and performing specialized legal reasoning tasks.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Thematic Analysis of Legal Documents
LLMs assist with the thematic analysis of legal texts, such as court opinions. They can generate initial coding for datasets, identify themes, and classify data, improving the efficiency and quality of legal research [466].

## Explanation Generation
When augmented with relevant case law, LLMs can generate high-quality explanations of legal terms with improved factual accuracy and less incorrect information [467].

## Legal Reasoning and Question Answering
LLMs can be trained with specialized domain knowledge to perform legal reasoning tasks and answer legal questions [468, 469].
