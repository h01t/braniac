# AlphaFold 3

**Summary**: AlphaFold 3 is an AI model developed by DeepMind for predicting molecular structures and interactions across various biomolecules, significantly accelerating drug discovery.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

## Overview
AlphaFold 3 is a computational biology model announced by Google DeepMind in May 2024. It extends the capabilities of its predecessor by predicting not just protein structures, but also the structures and interactions of a wide range of biomolecules, including DNA, RNA, and ligands (Source refs: 70, 71).

## Capabilities and Impact
The model is designed to model molecular complexes, which is a critical step in understanding biological processes and designing new drugs. Its high accuracy is credited with significantly accelerating drug discovery timelines, enabling the first AI-designed drugs to enter clinical trials as early as 2025 (Source refs: 70, 71). This represents a major frontier for [[concepts/artificial-intelligence.md]] in scientific research.

## Development Context
AlphaFold 3 is part of Google DeepMind's broader portfolio of AI research, which includes scaling compute-heavy models and integrating AI into enterprise ([[entities/vertex-ai.md]]) and consumer products. It builds upon the foundational work of the original [[concepts/alphafold.md]] system.
