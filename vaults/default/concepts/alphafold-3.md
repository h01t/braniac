# AlphaFold 3

**Summary**: A deep learning model developed by DeepMind for predicting the 3D structure of proteins and other biomolecules, with high accuracy.
**Source Context**: DeepMind publications (2024).

---

AlphaFold 3 expands on its predecessor by modeling interactions between proteins, DNA, RNA, and small molecules. It uses a diffusion-based architecture to generate atomic coordinates.

## Significance
- **Accuracy**: Achieves state-of-the-art results in the CASP competition.
- **Impact**: Accelerates drug discovery and biological research.
