# Applications of LLMs in Robotics

**Summary**: LLMs are applied in robotics to enhance human-robot interaction, task and motion planning, navigation, object manipulation, and to enable continuous learning for skill acquisition.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview ofLLMs.pdf

---

## Core Robotic Functions
LLMs have promising applications in enhancing human-robot interaction [472, 473, 474], task planning [237], motion planning [246], navigation [475], and object manipulation [236].

## Understanding and Collaboration
LLMs enable robots to understand their environment effectively and generate plans to complete tasks collaboratively with humans [240, 26].

## Continuous Learning
By accessing and integrating information from wide-ranging sources, LLMs facilitate continuous learning for robots, helping them acquire new skills, adapt to changes, and refine their operation [224, 233, 234].
