# Educational Benchmarks
Various datasets used for assessing knowledge and reasoning in education and AI, as referenced in the PDF.

## List of Benchmarks
- **BBH**: Multiple-choice questions from Chinese education exams.
- **C-Eval**: Language understanding evaluation in Chinese.
- **C3**: Comprehension benchmark.
- **CCPM**: Mathematics problem-solving.
- **CMATH**: Mathematics questions.
- **CMMLU**: Multiple-choice language understanding.
- **DROP**: Reading comprehension with options.
- **CHID**: Coreference resolution in Chinese.
- **CLUEWSC**: Word sense choice.
- **GSM8K**: Grade school math problems.
- **HumanEval**: Programming problems.
- **MATH**: Mathematical problem-solving.
- **MBPP**: Programming benchmarks.
- **MMLU**: Massive Multi-task Language Understanding.
- **NaturalQuestions**: Open-domain question answering.
- **OpenBookQA**: Science questions.
- **PIQA**: Physical interaction question answering.

## Purpose
These benchmarks test diverse skills like logic, language, math, and programming.
