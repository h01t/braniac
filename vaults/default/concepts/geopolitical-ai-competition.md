# Geopolitical AI Competition

**Summary**: The strategic contest between nations, primarily the U.S. and China, for leadership and dominance in artificial intelligence technology, a context in which OpenAI actively positions itself.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## OpenAI's Stance and Actions
[[entities/openai.md]] positions itself as advancing U.S. technological leadership in the global AI competition (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI). It complies with U.S. export controls by restricting access to its technologies in countries like China, Russia, and Iran.

## Measures Against China
OpenAI enforces strict access limits in China, blocking services for mainland users since mid-2024 and disrupting accounts tied to Chinese government entities in 2025 that sought to use its models for surveillance and influence operations (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI). CEO Sam Altman has warned that the U.S. underestimates Beijing's AI progress and independent scaling capabilities (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

## "OpenAI for Countries" Initiative
In 2025, OpenAI launched this initiative to provide customized AI infrastructure and training to U.S. allies pursuing "sovereign AI," aiming to counter China's open-source model distribution in the Global South and foster Western-aligned ecosystems (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
