# Superalignment

**Summary**: A research program at OpenAI aiming to align superintelligent AI systems (exceeding human-level capability) within four years, dedicating 20% of compute resources.

## Background
Announced in 2023, the superalignment team was led by Ilya Sutskever and Jan Leike. The team was dissolved in 2024 after leadership departures.
