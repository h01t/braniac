# Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

**Summary**: A hypothetical type of artificial intelligence that possesses the ability to understand, learn, and apply its intelligence to solve any problem across a wide range of domains, much like a human being.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI (referenced in the company's founding mission)

---

AGI, sometimes called "strong AI," is distinguished from today's "narrow AI," which excels at specific tasks (like playing chess or generating text). AGI would imply flexible, general-purpose reasoning and cognitive abilities.

## Relation to OpenAI's Mission
The pursuit of AGI is central to [[entities/openai.md]]'s stated mission "to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity." The company's research and development of increasingly capable [[concepts/gpt-models.md]] are seen as stepping stones toward this long-term goal. The potential arrival of AGI is the primary motivation behind the company's focus on [[concepts/ai-safety-alignment.md]] research, due to the existential risks a misaligned superintelligent system could pose.

## Debate and Timeline
There is significant debate within the AI community about if and when AGI might be achieved. Definitions vary, and some argue that current LLMs show sparks of generality. The focus on AGI also drives criticism that companies like OpenAI may be overhyping capabilities or diverting attention from the immediate, tangible harms of present-day AI systems.
