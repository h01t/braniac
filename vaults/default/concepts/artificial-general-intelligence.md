# Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

**Summary**: A hypothetical type of artificial intelligence that possesses the ability to understand, learn, and apply its intelligence to solve any problem, much like a human being. It is the stated core mission and goal of OpenAI.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## OpenAI's Mission
The core strategic goal of [[entities/openai.md]] is to achieve AGI and ensure it benefits all of humanity (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI). This mission entails substantial, long-term investments in compute infrastructure and research.

## Commercial Implications
The partnership agreement between OpenAI and [[entities/microsoft.md]] includes provisions that extend Microsoft's exclusive API access and IP rights "until AGI is achieved," indicating AGI is a defined contractual milestone (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
