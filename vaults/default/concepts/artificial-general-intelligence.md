# Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

**Summary**: A hypothesized AI system that can understand, learn, and apply its intelligence to solve any problem at a level equal to or surpassing a human across a wide range of economically valuable tasks.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

**Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)** refers to a level of artificial intelligence that possesses the ability to understand, learn, and perform any intellectual task that a human being can. This contrasts with "narrow" AI, which is designed for specific tasks. The concept is central to the mission of [[entities/openai.md]], which defines AGI as systems that "surpass humans at most economically valuable work" (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

The pursuit of AGI is cited by OpenAI's founders as the primary motivation for starting the organization. They expressed concern that profit-driven development by large corporations could lead to unsafe AGI, prompting them to establish a non-profit entity dedicated to its safe and beneficial development (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

The technical and safety challenges associated with developing AGI are immense. OpenAI's early research included foundational work on [[concepts/ai-safety.md]] to address issues like reward hacking and safe exploration, which are seen as critical for aligning future AGI systems with human values (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
