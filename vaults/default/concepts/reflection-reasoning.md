# Reflection in Reasoning

**Summary**: A reasoning behavior where a model revisits and reevaluates its previous steps or initial approach to a problem, often leading to correction or deeper analysis.
**Source Context**: DeepSeek_R1.pdf

---

## Emergent Behavior
[[concepts/reflection-reasoning.md|Reflection]] is identified as a sophisticated reasoning behavior that emerges spontaneously during the [[concepts/self-evolution.md]] of [[entities/deepseek-r1-zero.md]] through [[concepts/reinforcement-learning-reasoning.md]] (Source: DeepSeek_R1.pdf). It is not explicitly programmed into the model.

## Manifestation in the "Aha Moment"
The documented "aha moment" for DeepSeek-R1-Zero is a prime example of reflection. An intermediate version of the model learned to allocate more thinking time to a problem by **reevaluating its initial approach**, a core reflective act (Source: DeepSeek_R1.pdf).

## Role in Advanced Reasoning
This behavior is a key component of complex problem-solving, allowing the model to explore alternative solutions and correct its own reasoning path. It contributes significantly to the model's growing ability to tackle challenging tasks (Source: DeepSeek_R1.pdf).
