# Benchmarking

**Summary**: The process of evaluating AI model performance against standardized tests, a practice criticized for potential conflicts of interest and selective reporting by developers like OpenAI.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

Benchmarking is crucial for assessing AI capabilities but has faced accusations of manipulation and lack of independence. A notable controversy involved OpenAI's [[entities/o3.md]] model in January 2025. OpenAI funded the creation of the "independent" FrontierMath dataset and was granted prior access before o3 set records on it, sparking claims of undue influence. AI researcher [[entities/gary-marcus.md]] called the promotional tactics "manipulative."

Further reports showed that o3 lagged in practical applications despite its benchmark performance, exposing a gap between standardized tests and real-world utility. The saturation of standard tests like MMLU has driven the creation of new metrics, which remain vulnerable to developer bias.

These issues highlight broader challenges in AI evaluation and feed into criticisms about [[concepts/transparency.md]] and corporate influence over the perceived progress of the field.
