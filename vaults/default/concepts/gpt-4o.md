# GPT-4o

**Summary**: A multimodal AI model released by OpenAI in May 2024 that processes and generates text, audio, and vision in real time.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

**GPT-4o** ("o" for "omni") is a multimodal model released by [[entities/openai.md]] on May 13, 2024. It can process and generate combinations of text, audio, and image inputs and outputs in real time, with OpenAI claiming voice response latencies as low as 320 milliseconds (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

The model was designed for more natural, integrated reasoning across modalities and included expressive voice interaction features. It initially launched for paid [[concepts/chatgpt.md]] users before being extended to free users for text and image tasks. GPT-4o was positioned as a step towards more fluid human-computer interaction (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
