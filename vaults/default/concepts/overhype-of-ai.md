# Overhype of AI

**Summary**: The accusation that AI companies, including OpenAI, exaggerate the capabilities and near-term prospects of their technology, potentially fueling investment bubbles and misdirecting policy.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

This concept refers to claims that the AI industry, led by figures like [[entities/sam-altman.md]], promotes unrealistic expectations about artificial intelligence. Critics point to Altman's predictions of achieving [[concepts/agi.md]] by 2025 and superintelligence by 2030 as examples.

The release of [[entities/gpt-5.md]] in 2025 was seen by some as underwhelming, offering only incremental gains rather than transformative advances promised by the hype. Researchers like [[entities/gary-marcus.md]] highlight persistent limitations in reasoning and reliability.

This overhype is linked to fears of an AI investment bubble, with Altman himself noting market irrationality similar to the dot-com era. It also intersects with criticisms of [[concepts/benchmarking.md]], where performance on specialized tests may not translate to practical utility, creating a perception gap.
