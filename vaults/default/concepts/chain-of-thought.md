# Chain-of-Thought (CoT)

**Summary**: A reasoning technique where the LLM generates a step-by-step explanation of its reasoning process before providing an answer.
**Source Context**: Fundamentals of Building Autonomous LLM Agents.pdf

---

## Use in Agents

CoT is used in the [[concepts/reasoning-system.md]] of LLM agents to break down complex problems into intermediate steps, mimicking human problem-solving.

## Benefits

CoT can improve the accuracy and transparency of reasoning, especially for multi-step tasks.

## Relation to Other Techniques

CoT is a foundational technique that has been extended to approaches like [[concepts/tree-of-thought.md]] (ToT).
