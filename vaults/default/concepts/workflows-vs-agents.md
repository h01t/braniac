# Workflows vs. Agents

**Summary**: A distinction between structured, sequential workflows and autonomous, adaptive agents in the context of LLM-powered systems.
**Source Context**: Fundamentals of Building Autonomous LLM Agents.pdf

---

## Workflows

Workflows are structured systems that enhance LLMs by enabling tool use, environmental interaction, or memory access. However, they follow a pre-established, fixed sequence of steps and are rigid. They perform well in controlled, predictable environments but struggle with errors or unexpected changes.

## Agents

Agents are autonomous systems that generate their own strategies and adapt based on environmental feedback. They use techniques like [[concepts/chain-of-thought.md]] and iterative refinement to handle dynamic and unpredictable tasks.

## Key Difference

The key difference is adaptability: agents can re-plan and adjust, while workflows cannot.
