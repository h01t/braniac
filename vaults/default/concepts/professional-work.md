# Professional Work Capabilities

**Summary**: Professional work capabilities involve an AI model's expertise in domains like finance, office software, and task delivery in work scenarios.

## Capabilities of MiniMax M2.7

M2.7 has enhanced abilities in handling Word, Excel, and PPT documents, with multi-round high-fidelity editing. It can read reports, build models, and produce deliverables like a junior analyst (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).

## Example: Financial Modeling

In a test with TSMC, M2.7 autonomously read annual reports, designed assumptions, built a revenue forecast model, and generated PPT and Word documents (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).

## Benchmark Performance

On [[concepts/benchmarks.md#gdpval-aa|GDPval-AA]], M2.7 achieved a high ELO score, and on [[concepts/benchmarks.md#mm-claw|MM Claw]], it scored close to Sonnet 4.6 (Source: https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-m27-en).
