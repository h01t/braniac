# Distillation of Reasoning Capability
A method to transfer strong reasoning capabilities from a large model (like [[entities/DeepSeek-R1.md]]) to smaller, more efficient open-source models (e.g., Qwen, Llama). This is achieved by directly fine-tuning the smaller models on a curated dataset of reasoning samples (e.g., ~800k samples) generated by the larger model.

The process typically involves only Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and not an additional RL stage, though incorporating RL could further boost performance. This technique demonstrates a significant enhancement in the reasoning abilities of the smaller distilled models.
