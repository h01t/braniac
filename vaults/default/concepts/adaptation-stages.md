# Adaptation Stages of LLMs

**Summary**: The stages through which LLMs are developed and utilized, from initial pre-training to fine-tuning and prompting for tasks.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf, Batch 2 of 15.

---

LLMs undergo several adaptation stages, depicted in Figure 6 of the source:

- **Pre-Training**: The first stage where models are trained in a self-supervised manner on large corpora to predict next tokens, using various [[concepts/training-objectives.md]] [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf].

- **Fine-Tuning**: Includes approaches like transfer learning, instruction-tuning, and alignment-tuning to adapt models for downstream tasks or human preferences [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf].

- **Prompting / Utilization**: Methods to query trained LLMs, such as zero-shot prompting, in-context learning, and reasoning techniques, enabling interaction without fine-tuning [Source: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf].

These stages are essential for deploying LLMs in real-world applications.
