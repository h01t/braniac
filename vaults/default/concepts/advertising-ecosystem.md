# Advertising Ecosystem

**Summary**: An overview of digital advertising platforms, programmatic ad buying, and the impact of AI on ad targeting and personalization.

## Key Components
- **Demand-Side Platforms (DSPs)**: Allow advertisers to buy ad impressions in real time.
- **Supply-Side Platforms (SSPs)**: Enable publishers to sell ad inventory programmatically.
- **Ad Exchanges**: Marketplaces where ad inventory is traded.
- **Ad Networks**: Aggregators of ad inventory from multiple publishers.

## AI in Advertising
AI models are used for audience segmentation, creative optimization, and fraud detection. Large language models can generate ad copy and personalize messaging at scale.
