# Advertising Ecosystem

**Summary**: Google's advertising ecosystem is a comprehensive platform that enables targeted ad delivery across its services (like Search and YouTube) and partner networks, generating the majority of the company's revenue.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

The [[concepts/advertising-ecosystem.md]] is the financial engine of [[entities/google-llc.md|Google]] and its parent [[entities/alphabet-inc.md]]. It operates on an "attention economy" model, offering free services to attract users, collecting data on their behavior, and using that data to sell highly targeted advertising.

## Core Components
*   **Google Ads (formerly AdWords)**: An auction-based platform where advertisers bid on keywords to display text, shopping, or display ads. Ad rank is determined by bid amount and a "Quality Score."
*   **Google AdSense**: Allows website publishers to display targeted Google ads on their sites and earn revenue.
*   **Display Network**: A vast network of over 2 million websites, videos, and apps where Google ads can appear.
*   **YouTube Advertising**: Includes formats like TrueView (skippable) and bumper ads. YouTube ad revenue was $8.9 billion in Q3 2024 (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).
*   **AdMob**: A platform for monetizing mobile apps with ads.

## Technology and AI
The ecosystem handles trillions of ad auctions daily. It uses advanced machine learning for automated bidding, audience targeting, and campaign optimization. Recent AI innovations include "Demand Gen" campaigns, which reportedly yield 26% better conversions per dollar (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google).

## Financial Dominance
Advertising historically accounts for 75-80% of Alphabet's total revenue, which reached $350 billion in 2024 (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google). This dominance has attracted significant antitrust scrutiny.
