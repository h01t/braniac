# Valuation

**Summary**: The estimated market value of OpenAI, which experienced rapid and dramatic growth from 2023 through early 2026, driven by generative AI demand and major funding rounds.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## Valuation Timeline
*   **Late 2023**: $90–100 billion.
*   **November 2024**: $157 billion (for both a $6.6B round and a secondary tender offer).
*   **October 2025**: $500 billion (for a $6.6B share sale).
*   **December 2025**: Thrive Capital invested at a $285 billion valuation, structured separately from the main funding round which was seeking a valuation up to $830 billion.
*   **February 27, 2026**: $730 billion pre-money valuation for a $110 billion funding round, resulting in an approximately $850 billion post-money valuation.
*   **Post-February 2026**: The valuation was projected to reach ~$850 billion post-money following subsequent capital additions.

## Ownership Structure (as of October 2025 at $500B valuation)
Key ownership included (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI):
*   Microsoft: ~27%
*   OpenAI Foundation: 26%
*   Employees: ~25%
*   2025 fundraise investors (including SoftBank): ~13%
*   Others: Smaller stakes.

**Note**: The massive February 2026 round diluted Microsoft's stake, though the higher valuation increased the absolute dollar value of its holding.
