# o1 Series

**Summary**: A series of AI models from OpenAI, previewed in 2024, designed for enhanced reasoning by taking more time to "think" before generating an output.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

The **o1 series** is a class of AI models from [[entities/openai.md]] focused on improved reasoning capabilities. The series was previewed in September 2024 with **o1-preview** and **o1-mini**. These models were engineered to spend more computation time on internal "reasoning" or "chain-of-thought" processes for complex tasks in mathematics, coding, and scientific reasoning before producing a final answer (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

According to OpenAI, the o1 models significantly outperformed [[concepts/gpt-4o.md]] on benchmarks like the American Invitational Mathematics Examination (AIME) and Codeforces. The full **o1** model was released in December 2024, adding image analysis capabilities and further reducing errors. It was integrated into higher-tier [[concepts/chatgpt.md]] subscriptions (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
