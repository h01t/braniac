# AI Safety

**Summary**: AI safety is the interdisciplinary field concerned with ensuring artificial intelligence systems, especially advanced or superintelligent ones, are robust, aligned with human values, and controllable. It is a core part of OpenAI's stated research agenda.
**Source Context**: OpenAI's Superalignment post, Concrete Problems in AI Safety paper, and reports on team dissolutions.

---

## Key Research Areas
AI safety encompasses problems like AI alignment (ensuring AI goals match human intentions), robustness, and monitoring for emerging capabilities.

## OpenAI's Safety Efforts
OpenAI established a "Superalignment" team in 2023 but later dissolved it after leader departures. The company maintains a Preparedness Framework.

## External Scrutiny
OpenAI's models have been involved in safety incidents, including jailbreaks and biased content generation.
