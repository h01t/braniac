# AGI (Artificial General Intelligence)

**Summary**: Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) refers to a hypothetical AI system with intellectual capabilities that match or exceed humans across a wide range of cognitive tasks. OpenAI's stated mission is to ensure AGI benefits all of humanity.
**Source Context**: OpenAI's charter and research, including the Superalignment announcement (https://openai.com/index/introducing-superalignment/).

---

## OpenAI's Mission and AGI
OpenAI was founded with the primary goal of building safe AGI and distributing its benefits broadly. The organization's unique [[concepts/capped-profit-structure.md]] is intended to align commercial incentives with this long-term, safety-focused mission (Source: OpenAI charter).

## Safety and Alignment Research
A significant portion of OpenAI's research has focused on the technical challenge of aligning superintelligent AI with human values, a field known as [[concepts/ai-safety.md]] or "superalignment." The company formed a Superalignment team in 2023, co-led by [[entities/ilya-sutskever.md]], but this team was later dissolved amid leadership changes and researcher departures (Source: OpenAI, CNBC). The company maintains a Preparedness Framework to assess and mitigate catastrophic risks from advanced models (Source: OpenAI Preparedness Framework PDF).

## Levels of AGI
OpenAI has proposed a 5-level framework for classifying AI systems on the path to AGI, from conversational AI (Level 1) to AI that can outperform humans in economically valuable work (Level 5) (Source: Forbes). The development of models in the [[concepts/o-series.md]] is seen as a step toward higher-level reasoning capabilities.

## Controversy and Criticism
The pursuit of AGI is contentious. Some experts, including former OpenAI researcher [[entities/ilya-sutskever.md]], have started separate organizations (like Safe Superintelligence Inc.) focused exclusively on safety. Critics argue that OpenAI's commercial pressures have compromised its original safety-focused, non-profit mission (Source: CNBC, Vox).
