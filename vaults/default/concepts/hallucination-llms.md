# Hallucination in Large Language Models

**Summary**: The tendency of large language models to generate confident but incorrect or fabricated information, posing reliability risks.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

Hallucination is a critical issue where LLMs produce outputs that are not grounded in factual data. Y. Zhang et al. provide a comprehensive survey on this topic, titled "Siren's song in the ai ocean: A survey on hallucination in large language models" [Source: arXiv:2309.01219]. This phenomenon can undermine trust in applications like human-robot collaboration, as studied by Y. Ye et al. [Source: Improved trust in human-robot collaboration with chatgpt].

Mitigating hallucination involves improving model training and evaluation, with related research on generalization by C. Zhang et al. [Source: Understanding deep learning (still) requires rethinking generalization] and memorization vs. generalization by M. T�nzer et al. [Source: Memorisation versus generalisation in pre-trained language models].
