# Distillation of Reasoning Capabilities

**Summary**: The process of transferring reasoning patterns from a large, powerful model to smaller models via fine-tuning, enabling the smaller models to achieve strong reasoning performance.
**Source Context**: DeepSeek_R1.pdf

---

## Process Overview

Distillation involves using the outputs (e.g., reasoning chains) generated by a large teacher model to fine-tune smaller student models. In the DeepSeek-R1 paper, the reasoning data from [[entities/deepseek-r1.md]] is used to distill capabilities into smaller dense models based on [[entities/qwen.md]] and [[entities/llama.md]] architectures.

## Key Finding

The paper finds that direct distillation from DeepSeek-R1 to smaller models yields better reasoning performance than applying RL directly on those smaller models. This indicates that the reasoning patterns discovered by the large model are crucial and transferable.

## Results of Distillation

The distilled models (ranging from 1.5B to 70B parameters) achieve state-of-the-art performance on reasoning benchmarks. For example:
- DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B scores 55.5% on AIME 2024, surpassing the larger QwQ-32B-Preview model.
- DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B scores 72.6% on AIME 2024 and 94.3% on MATH-500, comparable to o1-mini.

These models are open-sourced to benefit the research community.

## Implications

This demonstrates an efficient path to creating powerful reasoning models at smaller scales, making advanced reasoning more accessible.
