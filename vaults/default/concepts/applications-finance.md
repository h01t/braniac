# Applications of LLMs in Finance

**Summary**: Domain-specific LLMs like BloombergGPT and FinGPT demonstrate superior performance on financial tasks, enabling applications in robo-advising, algorithmic trading, and complex task breakdown.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Domain-Specific Models
LLMs like [[entities/bloomberggpt.md]] (trained on proprietary financial data) and FinGPT (an open-source model) show the value of domain-specific training. They more accurately understand and process industry-specific language and concepts [151, 470].

## Novel Financial Applications
These models enable novel applications such as robo-advising, algorithmic trading, and low-code solutions, expanding the capabilities of financial services [470].

## Complex Task Breakdown
LLMs demonstrate an ability to break down complex financial tasks into actionable plans, enabling end-to-end solutions that were previously unfeasible with a single model [471].
