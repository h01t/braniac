# Academic and Specialized LLMs in China

**Summary**: Alongside corporate releases, China's open-source LLM ecosystem includes significant contributions from universities and research institutes, as well as a proliferation of domain-specific models fine-tuned for fields like law, medicine, education, and finance.
**Source Context**: https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/chinese-open-source-llms-2025

---

## Notable Academic Models
*   **Fudan University – MOSS**: Announced in April 2023 as “the first open-source conversational language model in China with plugin enhancements,” offered via a public API under an Apache-2 license [^28].
*   **Tsinghua University – ChatGLM Foundation**: The research groups at Tsinghua laid the groundwork for the [[entities/zhipu-ai.md]] ChatGLM models, with early models like GLM-130B published in 2023 [^29].
*   **Beijing Academy of AI (BAAI) – Aquila Series**: The non-profit BAAI has developed and open-sourced models like **Aquila-7B** for code generation and **AquilaChat-7B** on Hugging Face, following an open science approach [^30].

## Domain-Specific Models
Dozens of Chinese teams have fine-tuned and open-released models for specialized domains, often based on existing open architectures like LLaMA or ChatGLM. Examples include:
*   **ChatLaw**: A legal assistant model.
*   **DoctorGLM**: A medical model from ShanghaiTech University.
*   **EduChat**: For education.
*   **TigerBot**: For financial questions.

## Ecosystem Role
These academic and specialized models enrich the [[concepts/chinese-open-source-llm-landscape-2025.md]], driving innovation in specific verticals and demonstrating the depth of China's open-source AI research beyond large corporate labs.
