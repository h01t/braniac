# GPT-4

**Summary**: A multimodal large language model released by OpenAI in 2023, capable of processing both text and image inputs.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

**GPT-4** is a large multimodal model released by [[entities/openai.md]] in 2023. Unlike its predecessors, it could accept both text and image inputs to generate text outputs. It represented another major advance in the [[concepts/gpt-series.md]], with improved reasoning capabilities and performance on professional and academic benchmarks (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).

GPT-4 powered the paid version of [[concepts/chatgpt.md]] (ChatGPT Plus) and was widely integrated into enterprise applications via OpenAI's API. Its development and training were supported by the expanded infrastructure from OpenAI's deepened partnership with [[entities/microsoft.md]] (source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI).
