# Initial Public Offering (IPO)

**Summary**: The potential future event where OpenAI would become a publicly traded company, a subject of ongoing speculation and preparation as of early 2026.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI

---

## OpenAI's IPO Status
As of March 2026, [[entities/openai.md]] remains a private company and has not conducted an Initial Public Offering (IPO) (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI). No IPO occurred in February 2026, and no formal S-1 has been filed.

## Preparations and Timeline
OpenAI has engaged in informal discussions with Wall Street banks and hired personnel like former DocuSign CFO Cynthia Gaylor for investor relations to prepare for a potential IPO (Source: https://grokipedia.com/page/OpenAI). Reports indicate a possible filing in the second half of 2026, with some speculation pointing to Q4 2026, though internal references from the CFO have suggested 2027 as a target. The company has stated that "an IPO is not our focus," but preparations appear active, partly driven by a perceived race with rival [[entities/anthropic.md]] to be the first major AI lab to go public.
