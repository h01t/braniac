# Gemini Robotics

**Summary**: The Gemini Robotics family of models, introduced by Google DeepMind in 2025, enables robots to perceive, reason, plan, and act in the physical world, supporting complex tasks and human-robot interaction.
**Source Context**: https://grokipedia.com/page/Google

---

## Overview
Gemini Robotics is a family of AI models for embodied intelligence and physical AI, introduced by Google DeepMind in 2025. These models are built on the [[concepts/gemini-architecture.md]] and are designed to be deployed on robots with diverse morphologies (Source refs: 72, 73).

## Capabilities
The models enable robots to perform complex multistep tasks by providing capabilities in spatial understanding, tool use, and human-robot interaction. They are a key development toward creating general-purpose physical AI agents that can operate in unstructured environments (Source refs: 72, 73, 74).

## Collaborations and Applications
A significant collaboration involves integrating these AI capabilities with Boston Dynamics' Atlas humanoid robot, enhancing its navigation and operational abilities (Source ref: 74). This partnership marks an expansion of DeepMind's impact from digital domains like games and biology into real-world [[concepts/robotics.md]] (Source ref: 75).

## Strategic Importance
Gemini Robotics represents a bridge between digital intelligence and physical interaction, complementing Google's broader AI strategy which includes scaling models and integrating them into products like Google Search and Assistant (Source context).
