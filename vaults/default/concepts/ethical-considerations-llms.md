# Ethical Considerations for Large Language Models

**Summary**: Key ethical issues in LLM development and deployment, including fairness, privacy, bias, and regulatory oversight, as addressed in recent research.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

Ethical considerations for LLMs encompass multiple dimensions. E. M. Bender et al. discuss the dangers of overly large models, termed "stochastic parrots," highlighting fairness and accountability [Source: On the dangers of stochastic parrots: Can language models be too big?]. Bias and toxicity in reasoning are examined by O. Shaikh et al. [Source: On second thought, let's not think step by step! bias and toxicity in zero-shot reasoning].

Privacy preservation is explored by H. Brown et al. [Source: What does it mean for a language model to preserve privacy?] and R. Plant et al. [Source: You are what you write: Preserving privacy in the era of large language models]. Regulatory oversight, particularly in healthcare, is emphasized by B. Mesk� et al.
