# AI Intellectual Property Disputes

**Summary**: Legal conflicts arising from the use of copyrighted data for training AI models, ownership of AI-generated works, and patent rights for AI inventions.

## Key Issues
- **Training Data Copyright**: Lawsuits against AI companies for using copyrighted works without permission.
- **Output Ownership**: Whether AI-generated content can be copyrighted.
- **Inventorship**: Whether AI systems can be named as inventors on patents.

## Notable Cases
Cases involving OpenAI, Stability AI, and others have shaped the legal landscape.
