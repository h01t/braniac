## Overview
The performance of [[entities/kimi-k2-5.md]] is evaluated on a wide range of benchmarks, organized along several capability axes as outlined in Section 5.1.1 of the report [2602.02276v1.pdf].

## Benchmark Taxonomy
The benchmarks are categorized as follows:

### Reasoning & General Knowledge
*   **Humanity's Last Exam (HLE)**
*   **AIME 2025**
*   **HMMT 2025 (Feb)**
*   **IMO-AnswerBench**
*   **GPQA-Diamond**
*   **MMLU-Pro**
*   **SimpleQA Verified**
*   **AdvancedIF**
*   **LongBench v2**

### Coding
*   **Competitive and Agentic Coding Benchmarks** (specific names not listed in provided chunk, implied by category).

### Multimodal Understanding
*   **Image Understanding Benchmarks**
*   **Video Understanding Benchmarks** (Performance is indicated in Table 4 of the report, not included in this chunk).

### Autonomous Agentic Execution & Computer Use
*   Benchmarks designed to test the model's ability to act as an autonomous agent and interact with computer interfaces, leveraging its [[concepts/parallel-agent-capability-induction.md]] training.

## Purpose
This comprehensive evaluation suite is designed to measure the model's proficiency across the full spectrum of capabilities it is designed for, from foundational reasoning and knowledge to advanced multimodal interaction and parallel agentic problem-solving.
