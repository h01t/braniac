# Bias and Fairness in LLMs

**Summary**: LLMs can inherit, amplify, and propagate societal biases present in their training data, leading to ethical concerns and unfair outcomes in their generated content.
**Source Context**: Comprehensive Overview of LLMs.pdf

---

## Source of Bias
The primary source of bias is the training data itself, which often reflects historical and societal inequalities. LLMs learn these patterns and can reproduce or even exaggerate them in applications ranging from hiring tools to clinical decision support [478].

## Manifestations and Concerns
Biases can manifest in various forms, such as stereotypical language, unequal representation, or skewed recommendations. This raises significant ethical and fairness issues, particularly when LLMs are deployed in sensitive domains like [[concepts/applications-medicine.md]], [[concepts/applications-law.md]], and [[concepts/applications-education.md]] [450, 451].

## Addressing the Challenge
Addressing bias involves curating more representative training datasets, developing debiasing techniques during training and inference, and implementing rigorous auditing and evaluation frameworks. It is a critical component of developing responsible and trustworthy AI.
