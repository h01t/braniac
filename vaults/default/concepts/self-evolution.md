# Self-Evolution

**Summary**: The autonomous improvement of a model's reasoning capabilities through reinforcement learning, where sophisticated problem-solving behaviors emerge without explicit programming.
**Source Context**: DeepSeek_R1.pdf

---

## Definition
In the context of [[entities/deepseek-r1-zero.md]], self-evolution refers to the process where a model's reasoning abilities improve intrinsically through its interaction with a [[concepts/reinforcement-learning-reasoning.md]] environment, without the influence of a supervised fine-tuning stage (Source: DeepSeek_R1.pdf).

## Mechanism
The process is driven by the model learning to leverage extended test-time computation, generating hundreds to thousands of reasoning tokens (Source: DeepSeek_R1.pdf). Key observed behaviors that emerge include [[concepts/reflection-reasoning.md]] and the exploration of alternative approaches to problems (Source: DeepSeek_R1.pdf). This evolution is evidenced by a consistent increase in the model's average "thinking time" or response length during training (Source: DeepSeek_R1.pdf).

## Significance
The "aha moment" observed in DeepSeek-R1-Zero is a prime example of self-evolution, demonstrating how RL incentives can lead to unexpected and sophisticated cognitive strategies (Source: DeepSeek_R1.pdf). This concept underscores the potential of RL to foster autonomous intelligence development in AI systems.
